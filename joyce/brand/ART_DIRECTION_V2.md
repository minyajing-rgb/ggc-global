## Visual direction: Strategic Systems, Human Spark

- Premium, future-facing, systems-led, international; not an ornamental luxury portrait or tourism poster.
- Clean white / very pale lavender base; deep violet typography; restrained champagne-gold lines and details. Royal blue and a small vivid magenta accent may express Joyce's energy.
- Structured grid, readable sans-serif body and navigation, confident display typography, generous space, light glass layers and deliberate depth.
- Systems motifs: connected modules, orbit/network structures, stages and pathways, transparent operating layers. Motion must be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Warmth comes from Joyce's actual smile, lively details, people and playful game-world cues; avoid a cold corporate clone or overwhelming neon/cyberpunk clutter.
- Games remain the business core: Social Casino/Casino+, Arcade, Sims. AI-native body/mind/spirit consumer products are a clearly labeled adjacent track, without medical or supernatural outcome promises.
- Global perspective through market/navigation structure and connections, not a wall of destination photographs.
- The desired sense of authority is conveyed through clear decisions, method, proof and composition; do not present astrological symbolism as evidence of professional capability.
