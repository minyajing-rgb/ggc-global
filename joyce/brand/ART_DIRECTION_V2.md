## System and content

The implemented website is real HTML with selectable text, responsive layout and working controls, not a flattened mockup image. It includes six interactive operating-map nodes, fourteen capability domains, twelve generalized illustrative business scopes and sixteen principal-led service prices.

Games remain primary: Social Casino/Casino+, Arcade and Simulation. AI-native Body, Mind & Spirit consumer products are an adjacent commercial product track, with no medical or supernatural outcome promises. Global perspective is expressed through markets, operating connections and publishing capability rather than destination collages.

Subtle floating elements respect reduced-motion preferences. Decorative network graphics are not live business data. Clear headings, readable body copy, focus states and bounded content panels take priority over ornamental microtype.
