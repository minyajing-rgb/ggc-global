## What is included

The English page expands beyond a short profile: career and attributed project evidence, three buyer entry paths, fourteen capability domains, sixteen scoped services, AI workflow examples, public pricing context, engagement boundaries, and a local email-brief builder.

The business hierarchy is games first: category/product strategy, analytics, LiveOps, economy/monetization, UA, global publishing, community/PR/distribution, organizational cadence, commercial diligence and partnerships. Web3 and AI have explicit scope and evidence limits.

## Pricing logic

CNY is the base proposed schedule. USD references use a fixed planning assumption of 7.20 CNY/USD, not a live exchange rate. The written scope controls billing currency and final fees. English OR Chinese delivery is included as agreed; duplicate bilingual production, recording rights, extra markets, travel, licenses, infrastructure and specialist implementation require scope.

- Decision call: CNY5,000.
- Executive diagnostic and memo: CNY20,000.
- Product/retention/monetization audit: fromCNY50,000.
- Strategic advisory: CNY30,000/month,6principalhours/month.
- Operating partner: fromCNY300,000/90days,4principaldays/month.
- Annual Copilot: fromCNY1,200,000/year,4principaldays/month.
- Executive AI workshop: fromCNY38,888/halfday.
- One-workflow AI pilot: fromCNY120,000.

These are not verified historical paid rates or a statistical market median. Archived Joyce proposals already contained20,000/single,~30,000/month and1,000,000+/year anchors. This page makes scope and responsibility explicit; it does not amend prior agreements or OpsGo checkout terms.

## Evidence and privacy

Public figures are attributed first-party career/project records. ChineseCityVille's reported700bps means7percentagepoints, not700%.500+meanscross-functionalcontributors, notdirectreports. Former employers and product names are not endorsements.

Do not promote legacy aggregate profit, mixed #1 rankings,7MDAU or exchange-listing variants without product/market/period/role reconciliation. Do not invent client testimonials, Web3 outcomes, funding results or AI productivity multiples.

There are no private customer names, relationship discussions or retrospective value ledgers on this page. Private business-work inventory is separately maintained in the authorized business knowledge repository. Do not publish the full private working XLSX here.

## Functionality and limits

- Service filters: advisory/projects, operating partner, AI.
- CNY/USD planning display.
- Scope buttons select a service in the intake form.
- Form validation creates a local text brief and a mailto draft.
- The form does NOT send email, store data, process payments or confirm bookings.
- No analytics or third-party tracking is installed by this page.
- Public page uses inline styles and JavaScript; no external framework dependency.
- No-JavaScript fallback includes the core prices and CSV link.
