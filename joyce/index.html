01

An operating question that will not go away

Unclear symptoms, inconsistent priorities, repeated ad-hoc requests.

A bounded decision session; assumptions, decision owner and follow-up scope.

02

A category or benchmark mismatch

The reference game, market or business model may not match the product.

A category-fit assessment and decision memo.

03

UA spend without a defensible growth case

Acquisition, retention and payback tell conflicting stories.

A read-only UA review with scoped commercial recommendations.

04

A product and its website tell different stories

Positioning, player proposition and commercial messaging do not align.

Product/positioning assessment and a prioritized brief.

05

The wrong role is being recruited

Title, seniority, responsibilities and evaluation criteria are unclear.

Role scorecards, JD calibration and an interview rubric.

06

An introduction without a fit case

A senior contact is requested before the purpose or value exchange is clear.

Partner research, a permission-based introduction brief and coordination.

07

A number looks persuasive but its definition does not

Annual versus cumulative; revenue versus profit; inconsistent currencies.

Source and metric-definition review for a bounded decision.

08

Adjacent game categories get treated as interchangeable

Social Casino, real-money products and machine-based gaming require distinct analysis.

Product/category and team-fit review; specialist questions where needed.

09

R&D is ready but the publishing layer is not

Markets, channels, launch ownership and commercial assumptions are disconnected.

A scoped publishing/GTM assessment or sprint.

10

The product ships but operating ownership disappears

Release, staffing and post-launch responsibilities are unclear.

Organization and handoff assessment; an operating mandate if required.

11

Metrics exist but commercial priorities do not

Data, economy and player lifecycle are reviewed in separate conversations.

A product, monetization or LiveOps review with agreed boundaries.

12

Expert access has become an unlimited inbox

Repeated advice has no scope, capacity ceiling or commercial agreement.

A prepaid, capacity-bounded advisory retainer and decision log.
