07 / How the engagement runs

Decide. Deliver. Review.

01 / Qualify

A product, a decision, an owner.

Share stage, market, KPI, data and budget. Confirm fit and conflicts. Agree scope, confidentiality and acceptance criteria.

02 / Operate

Turn judgment into work.

Establish a baseline, prioritized backlog, owners and decision rights. Identify who implements each recommendation.

03 / Review

Measure what changed.

Separate intervention, result and alternative explanations. Keep a decision log. Renew, change scope or stop on evidence.

Payment and communication

Proposed standard: calls/workshops prepaid; projects 50% kickoff and 50% handoff; retainers monthly in advance. Agreed business hours and acknowledgement targets apply. No unlimited messaging or emergency coverage.

Client responsibilities

Provide an accountable sponsor, authorized product/data access, reliable source definitions and an implementation owner. Media, travel, infrastructure, licenses and execution staff are separately budgeted.

Optional performance participation

Selected mandates only. A separate agreement must define baseline, incremental contribution, attribution, costs, refunds, reporting access, settlement and caps. No revenue, funding or ranking guarantee.

Deeper casebook and confidential evidence

Available through qualification and confidentiality controls. Each useful case states the problem, actual role, intervention, period, KPI definition and limitations. Unverified aggregate profit and mixed ranking claims are not public proof.

Introductions and access

No contacts are sold and no professional's time or endorsement is guaranteed. Research, fit assessment, outreach preparation and permission-based coordination can be scoped.

Language, scope and rights

One agreed language, English or Chinese, is included. Duplicate bilingual deliverables, recording or redistribution rights, extra markets and extended support require written scope. Final price and terms are set before work.
