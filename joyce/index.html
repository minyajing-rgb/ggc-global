06 / AI enablement

More creative capacity.
Less operating friction.

AI is useful when it improves a real workflow. A bounded pilot has authorized inputs, evaluation criteria, human approval and a named owner — not an invented productivity multiple.

Research → decision

Intelligence that stays attached to its source.

Category intelligence, competitor briefs and decision support. Pilot evaluation: accuracy, freshness, review effort and time per useful output.

Content → learning

Creative work with an operating feedback loop.

Briefs, localized variants and review assistance. Pilot evaluation: production cycle, rework, compliance issues and test coverage.

Data → action

Less reporting theatre. More usable context.

KPI commentary, anomaly triage and management briefs. Pilot evaluation: exception quality, analyst workload and follow-through.

Web3 scope covers game/product/commercial assessment, not token brokerage, investment returns or smart-contract security certification. AI pilots are not medical services, security audits or automatic production-readiness guarantees.

09 / How we work

A useful scope.
A clear next step.

01 / Qualify

Clarify the decision.

Confirm product, market, ownership, data, budget and fit.

02 / Engage

Agree the commitment.

Define deliverables, capacity, confidentiality and acceptance.

03 / Review

Make the next decision.

Review delivery and evidence; renew, rescope or close the mandate.

Payment & communication

Calls and workshops are prepaid. Proposed project terms: 50% kickoff / 50% handoff. Retainers are monthly in advance. Business hours, response targets and capacity are agreed; there is no unlimited messaging or emergency coverage.

Client inputs & decision rights

A named sponsor, authorized access, clear data definitions and an implementation owner are required. The scope identifies decisions GGC can make and decisions retained by the client.

Introductions & confidential cases

Research, fit assessment and permission-based coordination can be commissioned. Contacts are not sold; meetings, hires or deals are not guaranteed. Client cases require evidence and explicit permission.

Performance participation

Available only by separate agreement defining baseline, incremental contribution, attribution, costs, refunds, reporting access and settlement. No guaranteed returns.

AI and specialist delivery

Production engineering, smart-contract audits, regulated gambling mathematics, medical, legal and tax services are not included. Appropriate specialist delivery must be separately arranged.

Language, recording & reuse

One agreed delivery language is included. Duplicate bilingual outputs, recording or redistribution rights, added markets and extended support require written scope.
