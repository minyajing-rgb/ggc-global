## Editorial rules

1. Use **Decision**, not Judgement.
2. Position Joyce above a single-function "game operations expert":
   - Global Game Operator
   - Founder / Owner
   - Decision Architect
3. Show the full chain:
   **Product × Commercial × Global × Organization × Governance × Capital × AI**
4. Separate two products:
   - **GGC Principal** = high-trust decision and operating support
   - **GGC Intelligence / Decision OS** = productized intelligence / reusable operating asset
5. The book is a public knowledge layer, not the whole business.
6. Public proof must remain scoped and conservative:
   - do not publish unresolved aggregate revenue/profit claims;
   - 500+ means cross-functional collaboration network, not direct reports;
   - confidential consulting cases remain anonymized.
7. The core moat is not "knowing many things." It is:
   **top-tier operating XP + cross-stage pattern recognition + key decision quality + system codification.**
8. Tone for mainland founders / EMBA / listed-company executives:
   - less creator/influencer language;
   - more operating, strategy, governance, P&L, capital-allocation and decision language.
9. "See Clear" can appear as a branded diagnostic layer, but public business language should be:
   **Rapid Pattern Recognition / Diagnostic Clarity / Root-Cause & Decision Point Identification.**
