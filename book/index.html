FIRST VALUE · FREE

Before spending more on a game, answer five questions.

A practical diagnostic exercise inspired by the book's product-review method. This short checklist is free to read; it is not the full ebook.

  1. What exact user problem is the game solving, and for whom?
  2. Which behavior proves a player got the first meaningful value?
  3. Where does the player drop off: first session, return, or first purchase?
  4. What are the true costs, net revenue and payback assumptions?
  5. What decision will the next product review make, with which evidence and owner?

Use these questions as a one-page team discussion before approving the next milestone.
