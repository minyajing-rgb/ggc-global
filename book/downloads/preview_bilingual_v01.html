第一部分 经营视角与共同语言

Part One Operating Perspective and Shared Language

开篇 为什么重新写这本书

Opening · Why I Am Rewriting This Book

我做游戏，关心的是一件很具体的事情：一群人把产品创造出来之后，怎样让它真正走到用户面前，成为愿意被反复选择、能够持续经营的东西。

这些年，我在Microsoft、Tencent、Zynga、Gameloft和创业公司之间，做过产品、数据、发行、运营与商业化，主导和统筹过多款全球、平台及细分品类第一的旗舰项目。我的工作从新项目立项开始，也经常回到成熟产品，寻找它的下一段价值；有时要解决产品问题，有时要重新接起数据、团队与经营体系。

所以我写“运营”，不会只列一张活动表。我更想讲：面对一个具体项目，我怎么看问题，怎么判断，怎么组织，怎么让结果发生，以及结果发生以后怎样知道它为什么发生。

第一次写书时，我希望把这些经历变成别人可以理解、可以动手用的内容。后来继续做项目，也继续做自己的产品，发现还有很多东西应该讲得更完整。原先没有展开的操作过程、跨品类的差异、跨地区的条件，以及工作里那些很难被一个岗位名称概括的部分，都需要重新放回去。

现在还多了一组新的现场：AI。

我正在把AI用于研究、创意、内容、代码、业务工作流和消费产品。它带来了新的速度，也暴露了新的问题：一份写得很完整的方案，是否已经成为可用产品？一张漂亮界面，是否有真实交互和数据？自动化说做完了，究竟交付在哪里？哪些数字来自真实测试，哪些只是目标和预测？

这些问题值得写，包括我自己的判断如何改变，包括系统怎样误解任务，也包括我怎样逐步把要求、检查和工作方式教给它。进行中的事情，就按进行中写；发生过的结果，给出它的条件和证据。读者可以看到一段实践是怎样走到现在的。

这本扩充版仍然从游戏经营出发。它会走进不同品类和地区，也会走进直播、社交、电商、教育、Web3，以及我自己的消费AI与身心灵产品探索。能够迁移的，是对体验、行为、经济、组织和反馈的理解；迁移到新的领域，还要重新认识那里的人和需求。

我希望读完之后，你能更清楚地回答三个问题：现在最应该做什么，为什么做它，怎样知道它真的起了作用。

至于把一个产品做到更大的舞台，往往就从把这三个问题做明白开始。

English

When I work on a game, I care about a practical question: after a team creates a product, how does it reach people and become something they repeatedly choose—a product that can be operated sustainably?

Across Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga, Gameloft and founder-led companies, my work has connected product, data, publishing, operations and commercialization. I have led and coordinated flagship titles reaching leadership positions globally, on platforms and within categories. The work begins with new-project decisions and often returns to established titles to find their next source of value. Sometimes that means changing the product; sometimes it means reconnecting data, teams and the operating foundation.

That is why a book about operations needs more than an event calendar. I want to explain how I examine a project, make judgments, organize delivery and understand why an outcome occurred.

The first book aimed to make that experience understandable and usable. Continued work on projects and owned products revealed material that needed fuller treatment: operating steps, category differences, market conditions and responsibilities that a job title cannot adequately describe.

A new field of practice has also arrived: AI.

I am using AI in research, creative, content, code, business workflows and consumer products. It creates speed and exposes new questions. Has a complete-sounding plan become a usable product? Does an attractive screen have real interaction and data? When automation claims completion, where is the delivery? Which numbers come from tests, and which are targets or forecasts?

These experiences belong in the book: how my judgment changes, how systems misunderstand tasks, and how I progressively teach them requirements, checks and ways of working. Ongoing work should remain visibly ongoing. Reported outcomes should retain their conditions and evidence. Readers should be able to see how a practice reached its current state.

The expanded edition still begins with game operations. It moves across categories and markets, then into streaming, social products, commerce, education, Web3 and my consumer-AI and reflective-product experiments. Understanding experience, behavior, economy, organization and feedback can transfer. Entering a new field still requires learning its people and needs.

I hope the book helps you answer three questions more clearly: what deserves action now, why, and how to know that the action made a difference.

Taking a product onto a larger stage often begins by answering those questions well.

第01章 游戏运营到底对什么负责

Chapter 01 · What Game Operations Is Accountable For

开场 / Opening

如果你去问十家公司“运营是干什么的”，很可能得到十种回答。有人说做活动，有人说客服，有人说发内容，还有人说负责收入。岗位名称一样，实际坐的位置却可能差很远。

我判断一个运营岗位，先看三个东西：它能看见什么，它能决定什么，它最终要对什么结果负责。只看岗位名称，很容易把执行工作、专业职能和产品经营混在一起。

我的工作横跨产品、数据、发行、商业化和团队协作。新项目要从立项跟到上线，成熟产品要找到下一段经营空间，多款产品同时推进时还要调动不同的人和资源。这样的工作需要一张完整的图：玩家为什么来，进来以后经历什么，为什么留下，为什么付费，团队怎样持续提供值得回来的内容。

这张图，就是本书所说的“大运营”视角。

English

Ask ten companies what operations does and you may receive ten answers: events, customer support, content, revenue. The title is the same; the position within the business can be very different.

I assess an operating role through three questions: what can it see, what can it decide, and which outcomes must it answer for? A title alone can blur execution, specialist functions and responsibility for the product business.

My work connects product, data, publishing, commercialization and teams. A new title requires decisions from greenlighting through launch. An established title needs its next operating opportunity. A portfolio requires coordination across people and resources. These responsibilities need a complete picture: why players arrive, what they experience, why they return and pay, and how the team keeps delivering reasons to come back.

That is the broad operating perspective used throughout this book.

1.1 运营的哲学三问：做什么、为什么、凭什么

接到“下个月收入要提高”的目标，先不要急着排活动。你需要把三问落到具体的产品上。

做什么，是确定工作对象。新玩家进不来，老玩家在离开，付费用户变少，还是商品和内容已经没有吸引力？同样是收入下降，后面的工作可能完全不同。

为什么，是确定目标与问题之间的关系。活动多了一场，并不等于收入多了一份。折扣可能让玩家提前购买，也可能把原价收入移到了折扣窗口。如果你不能说明行动准备改变哪一段行为，就还没有回答为什么。

凭什么，是把判断变成能够接受检查的假设。依据可以是历史数据、同类产品的机制、玩家访谈、实际体验或一个小规模试验。每种依据能支持的判断范围不同：有人说喜欢，说明体验获得了一个正面反馈；真正再次登录，才说明回访发生了；付款与后续消费，又是下一层证据。

我习惯把一项工作写成一句能落地的话：面向哪一群玩家，在什么场景改变什么体验，希望哪个指标在什么窗口发生怎样的变化，由谁实现，用什么方式检查。把这句话写清楚，团队才知道自己在解同一个问题。

English

When asked to increase next month’s revenue, do not begin with an event calendar. Apply the three questions to the specific product.

What defines the object of the work. Are new players failing to arrive, existing players leaving, payer numbers falling, or offers and content losing appeal? The same revenue decline can require very different actions.

Why connects the action to the problem. One more event does not automatically create additional revenue. A discount may bring purchases forward or move full-price purchases into a discounted window. An action needs a clear explanation of the behavior it is intended to change.

On what basis turns judgment into a hypothesis that can be examined. Historical data, comparable mechanisms, player interviews, direct play and small tests each support different kinds of claims. Positive feedback establishes that someone liked an experience. A return visit establishes that a return occurred. Payment and repeat spending establish something further.

I write the task in one executable sentence: which players, which situation, which experience to change, which metric and time window, who will deliver it, and how it will be checked. That sentence gives the team a common problem to solve.

1.2 大运营与小运营：范围、权限与结果

“小运营”描述的是较窄的工作范围：执行活动、配置商品、发公告、处理玩家反馈、维护某个渠道。每件事都可能直接影响玩家体验，做好它需要专业能力。

“大运营”承担的是跨环节的经营责任。它需要把需求、产品、市场、数据和资源串起来，决定当前最值得推动的事情。有时要与制作人讨论系统，有时要与市场重新定义用户，有时问题来自发布流程，有时要先解决团队交接。

这两个称呼不表示谁更值得尊重。真正要分清的是权责。如果一个人被要求对产品收入负责，却拿不到分渠道数据，不能参与商品和活动设计，也不能协调研发时间，组织就给了他一个没有完整工具的目标。

在我的“大运营”视角里，研发、制作人及其美术、技术、策划团队，与游戏内、游戏外运营要放到同一张经营图里。具体团队中谁对哪个决定负责，还要写清楚；经营范围的连接，不等于所有人都向运营汇报。

English

A narrower operating remit might cover event execution, offer configuration, announcements, support or a channel. Each can materially affect players and requires professional skill.

A broad operating remit connects requirements, product, market, data and resources. It identifies the most valuable intervention at the current stage. That may mean discussing a system with a producer, redefining the audience with marketing, fixing a release process or resolving a team handover.

The distinction is about responsibility and authority. Someone accountable for revenue needs access to channel data, a role in offer and event decisions, and a way to coordinate development capacity. Without those interfaces, the organization has assigned a target without the means to deliver it.

My broad-remit perspective places development and its producer, art, engineering and design teams alongside operations inside and outside the game. These functions need to be connected. Their actual decision rights and reporting lines must still be specified.

1.3 游戏内与游戏外：把用户旅程连起来

游戏内的工作包括玩法体验、活动、经济、内容、社交、付费、客服与反馈。游戏外的工作包括市场定位、品牌、渠道、素材、商店包装、用户获取、公关与合作。不同公司的组织划分会变，但玩家经历的是一条连续的路径。

玩家先看见一段广告或一条朋友分享，形成一个预期。到了商店，他再次判断这是不是自己想玩的东西。安装、打开、加载、完成第一次操作、获得一次反馈，才真正走进产品。任何一段承诺和体验断开，都会把前一段的努力损耗掉。

因此，市场说“人已经带来了”和研发说“功能已经做好了”，还不能共同证明事情完成了。还要看这些人是不是目标玩家，进来以后能不能得到素材所承诺的体验，新老玩家在同一个经济里是否都能继续玩。

我反复强调，给新玩家导量时，也要照顾老玩家。新手礼包可能提高新人的进入意愿，却让老玩家觉得之前的投入被轻视。你要同时考虑两群人的体验，而不是把新增数字做完就交差。

把路径连起来之后，判断也更准确。商店没有转化，先看展示、预期、设备与进入成本；首轮体验掉得快，再查用户匹配、加载、引导和核心反馈；能留下却不付费，需要继续检查需求、商品、经济与付款流程。每一步都是待验证的原因，不能只凭一个漏斗位置定罪某个团队。

English

Work inside the game covers experience, events, economy, content, social systems, payment, support and feedback. Work outside covers positioning, brand, channels, creative, store presentation, acquisition, PR and partnerships. Organization charts differ, but players experience a continuous journey.

An advertisement or a friend’s post creates an expectation. The store page either strengthens or weakens it. Installation, opening, loading, the first action and its feedback determine whether the player actually enters the experience. A break between promise and delivery wastes the work that came before it.

“We brought the users” and “we completed the feature” are separate outputs. Together they still do not establish a successful journey. The audience must fit the product, receive the promised experience and coexist with existing players within its economy.

I emphasize the treatment of existing players when acquiring new ones. A starter offer may help newcomers but leave established players feeling that their earlier investment has been overlooked. Both experiences belong in the decision.

Connecting the journey improves diagnosis. Weak store conversion suggests examining presentation, expectations, devices and entry cost. Early departure suggests testing audience fit, loading, onboarding and core feedback. Players who stay but do not pay require investigation of need, offers, economy and checkout. These are hypotheses to test, not verdicts against a department.

1.4 研发阶段的运营介入与商业判断

很多经营问题在上线之前就已经形成了。内容消耗得比生产快，长期目标不够，经济只有产出没有合适的消耗，付费点脱离玩法，活动必须每次改代码才能做。这些问题到放量之后才发现，修复成本和组织压力都会更大。

运营早介入，要先帮助团队回答产品问题：这个体验面向谁？玩家愿意在这里付出时间的理由是什么？我们希望他每次玩多久，多久回来？体验怎样形成长期目标？哪一种商业模式与它相配？团队持续供给内容的速度够不够？

一个模拟经营产品里，“建造一栋房子”是玩法动作。建造消耗什么、产出进入哪里、仓储有没有上限、建筑如何升级、装饰提供什么价值、玩家是否需要别人帮助，则构成它能持续运转的系统。这些需要与策划和制作人一起推敲。

我不会把“越早介入”理解成越早往产品里塞商品。玩家必须先体验到值得投入的东西，付费才有落点。经营参与设计，是让体验、持续供给和商业目标相互支持。

到了AI原型阶段，同样要问这些问题。一个可以打开的Demo证明某段交互被做出来了；它还没有回答谁会反复使用、需要多少持续内容、实际付款为什么发生。生成得快，会让团队更快抵达这些问题，不会让这些问题消失。

English

Many operating problems are formed before launch: content consumed faster than it can be produced, weak long-term goals, excessive resource creation without suitable uses, offers disconnected from gameplay, or events requiring new code every time. Discovering them after expansion makes repairs more costly and organizationally difficult.

Early operating involvement begins with product questions. Who is the experience for? Why would players spend time in it? How long should a session last, and when should they return? How are long-term goals formed? Which business model fits, and can the team sustain the required content supply?

In simulation, building a house is an action. Its costs, outputs, storage limits, upgrades, decoration value and social dependencies form a sustainable system. These decisions need joint work with design and production.

Early involvement means aligning experience, ongoing supply and commercial goals. Players need something worth investing in before payment has a meaningful place.

AI prototypes raise the same questions. A playable demo establishes that an interaction exists. It does not establish repeated use, the cost of continued content or a reason to pay. Faster generation brings a team to these questions sooner; it does not remove them.

1.5 从单项KPI到产品整体经营

单个指标有用，但它要放回产品里。新增提高而留存下降，可能是在用更多人弥补更快的流失。付费率提高而总收入下降，可能是用户规模变小，也可能是原来的商品结构被改坏了。短期流水提高，还要看折扣、投放、平台分成、内容成本和后续回访。

我看一份运营计划，会检查它有没有把动作、结果和资源连起来。准备做什么，影响哪些玩家，动哪一个输入，预期何时看见变化，用掉多少研发与市场资源，出现什么副作用，什么情况下停止或调整。

这些问题不要求每个人都成为财务或程序专家，但要求产品经营者能把决定接起来。指标用于帮助团队判断，而不是让每个部门单独证明自己赢了。

还要写清责任边界。运营可以对方案、协调与跟进负责；整体结果往往同时依赖产品、技术、内容、市场和资源。明确共同目标与各自输入，才能真正协作，也才能在结果不好时找到下一步。

English

A metric becomes useful when returned to its product context. More acquisition with weaker retention may compensate for faster churn. Higher payer conversion with lower revenue may reflect a smaller audience or a damaged offer structure. A short-term revenue increase must be assessed alongside discounts, acquisition spending, platform deductions, content cost and later return behavior.

An operating plan should connect actions, outcomes and resources: which players it affects, which input changes, when an effect should appear, the development and marketing capacity required, potential side effects, and conditions for stopping or adapting.

The operator needs to connect decisions across specialist functions. Metrics help a team decide together. Ownership must be explicit: the operator owns particular decisions, coordination and follow-through, while results also depend on product, engineering, content, marketing and resources. Shared outcomes and defined inputs make collaboration—and diagnosis—possible.

1.6 技术、产品、数据、市场的共同语言

“优化一下”“做得高级一点”“提升体验”，都还没有到可以交付的程度。先把它翻译成玩家行为，再写成系统和界面需要做的事情，最后写出检查方法。

一个完整的运营需求要包含目标用户、目的、预期效果、流程、交互、界面草图、素材、文案、数值表和边界场景。还要区分优先级，让团队知道时间紧时哪几项必须完成。

比如“让新玩家参加活动”，至少要说明入口何时出现、哪些用户能看见、第一次点击后进入哪里、未达到条件时怎样提示、奖励何时发、失败或断网怎么办、完成与领取分别记录哪个事件。只有一个活动标题，无法替代这些内容。

同一标准也适用于AI参与的工作。生成了一份方案后，检查目标、结构、流程、界面和边界是否互相对应；生成了一段代码后，检查实际行为和数据记录是否符合方案。写得多不是验收标准，关键是该发生的事情是否真的发生。

选你正在负责的一款产品，回答：

1. 我对哪三个结果负责？/ Which three outcomes do I own? 2. 为了影响它们，我能控制或协调哪些输入？/ Which inputs can I control or coordinate? 3. 玩家旅程里，目前哪个交接最容易断？/ Which handoff is most fragile in the player journey? 4. 选一项本周工作，写出用户、场景、动作、指标、时间、负责人和验收。/ Specify audience, situation, action, metric, time window, owner and acceptance for one task this week.

写完后，把答案交给一个与你配合的人。请他复述：你准备解决什么，以及他需要交付什么。如果两个人的说法对不上，先把目标对齐，再进入下一轮执行。

English

“Optimize it,” “make it premium” and “improve the experience” are not yet deliverable requirements. Translate them into player behavior, then system and interface behavior, then an acceptance method.

A complete operating specification includes audience, objective, expected effect, flows, interactions, interface sketches, assets, copy, economy tables and edge cases. Priorities show what must survive a tight schedule.

“Get new players into an event” requires details: when the entry appears, eligibility, destination, failure messages, reward delivery, disconnection behavior, and separate completion and claim events. A title cannot replace these requirements.

AI work needs the same standard. A generated plan must connect objectives, structure, flows, interfaces and edge cases. Generated code must deliver the specified behavior and records. Acceptance depends on what actually happens.

After completing the exercise, ask a colleague to restate your problem and their deliverable. If the accounts differ, align them before the next execution cycle.

第02章 与制作人、策划、技术和市场一起做成事

Chapter 02 · Working with Producers, Designers, Engineers and Marketing

开场 / Opening

游戏是集体的创造，也是集体的交付。一群能力很强的人坐在一起，仍然可能做不成一件事情：每个人的任务都完成了，玩家得到的体验却是断的；每个人都知道目标，资源却没有落到同一个时间窗口。

我在Zynga作为PM，带着多位产品负责人和制作人推进多款旗舰项目。这样的工作会让人很快明白，职位名称不能代替协作机制。你需要知道各自的专业输入是什么，哪个决定要共同做，卡住时谁能把事情往前推。

运营的一个关键能力，是把不同团队的语言翻译成同一个产品结果。翻译之前，要先听懂。

English

A game is a collective creation and a collective delivery. A group of highly capable people can still fail to deliver a coherent experience: every task is complete, yet the player journey is broken; everyone knows the target, yet resources do not meet in the same release window.

At Zynga, I worked as a PM, leading work with multiple product managers and producers across flagship projects. A title cannot substitute for a collaboration mechanism. People need to understand each function’s contribution, which decisions are shared, and who can move a blocked decision forward.

An important operating capability is translating specialist languages into one product outcome. First, you must understand those languages.

2.1 游戏团队与App团队的组织差异

我画过游戏公司与App公司的组织图。游戏团队常围绕制作人、主程、主策、主美组织，App团队可能围绕产品与技术负责人组织。它们反映的是我接触到的团队形式，不是一套所有公司必须照抄的标准。

组织形式要跟交付对象一起看。游戏体验同时依赖玩法、经济、表现、内容和工程。一个系统改动，可能影响资源循环、战斗节奏、美术制作、服务器和活动配置。单独看需求表，容易低估它牵动了多少人。

小团队里，一个人可能兼几种职能；大型团队里，一个职能可能被拆得很细。兼岗并不意味着对应的检查可以消失，分工细也不意味着整体体验自动有人负责。

我看组织图，会再补一张交付图：谁提供输入，谁做决定，谁制作，谁检查，谁上线，谁在上线后观察结果。组织图告诉你人在哪里，交付图才告诉你事情怎么穿过去。

English

I have compared game and app organization structures. Game teams often center on a producer and engineering, design and art leads; app teams may center on product and engineering leadership. These diagrams describe structures I encountered rather than a universal template.

Read the organization alongside what it delivers. A game depends on gameplay, economy, presentation, content and engineering together. One system change may affect resource circulation, combat pace, art production, servers and event configuration.

In a small team, one person may hold several functions. In a large team, a function may be divided among specialists. Combined roles do not remove checks, and specialization does not automatically create ownership of the whole experience.

I supplement an organization chart with a delivery map: who provides inputs, decides, produces, checks, releases and observes results. The first locates people; the second explains how work moves through them.

2.2 制作人、策划、程序、美术、QA与平台支持

制作人需要把创作目标、范围、团队和资源接起来。策划把玩法与系统写成可以实现的结构。技术负责让它正确、稳定地运行。美术决定玩家看见和感受到的表现，QA帮助验证行为与风险，运维保障上线后的运行条件。

数据、客服、公关、商务与市场也可能是项目内或公共支持职能。不要因为不坐在同一间办公室，就到上线前才去找他们。需要什么能力，什么时候需要，要在排期里占一个位置。

与美术讨论活动时，运营不能只说“图漂亮一点”。要说明目标玩家、主题、画面使用位置、设备尺寸、文字层级、素材变体和交付日期。与QA讨论时，也不能只说“测一下”。要说明哪些条件、奖励、异常和数据记录决定活动能否上线。

职能 / Function协作需要说清的事 / What to clarify
制作 / Production范围、取舍、档期、资源 / Scope, trade-offs, schedule, resources
策划 / Design规则、循环、数值、解锁与目标 / Rules, loops, economy, progression, goals
技术与运维 / Engineering and operations实现、性能、稳定性、配置与恢复 / Implementation, performance, stability, configuration, recovery
美术 / Art表现目标、界面与素材规格 / Experience intent, interfaces, asset specifications

市场与商务 / Marketing and partnerships用户、承诺、渠道、档期与依赖 / Audience, promise, channels, schedule, dependencies

English

Production connects creative intent, scope, people and resources. Design specifies playable rules and systems. Engineering makes them work correctly and reliably. Art shapes what players see and feel. QA verifies behavior and risks; technical operations sustains the live environment.

Analytics, support, PR, partnerships and marketing may belong to the project or to shared teams. Their capacity must appear in the schedule before launch.

An art brief needs audience, theme, placement, device dimensions, text hierarchy, variants and delivery dates. A QA brief needs the conditions, rewards, exceptions and data records that determine release readiness. “Make it beautiful” and “test it” leave the important work unspecified.

2.3 运营与策划：玩法、数值与经营目标

模拟经营的例子很适合讲这个配合。策划提出收菜、建房子和功能建筑，运营继续追问：收来的东西去了哪里？仓库多大？建筑升级消耗什么？资源一直积累，玩家还有没有新的目标？

这些问题会连成一个经济系统。产出需要有用途，消耗需要有价值，升级需要让玩家感到进步，解锁需要形成期待。一级货币、付费货币、材料、声望并不是越多越好；每一种都应该有明确的行为位置。

在这里，时间和钱需要一起算。一个玩家不付费，要用多久完成阶段目标？愿意付费的玩家得到怎样的体验？他付出的金额，是在购买装饰、便利、选择、内容还是速度？两条路径怎样在同一个世界里相处？

所以运营参与经济设计，不只是提出“多挖一点付费空间”。要看需求从哪里来，玩家能不能理解价值，长期内容能不能支撑承诺，以及调整是否损害已有投入。

协作时，可以先画核心循环，再画扩展循环和资源流向。新功能要放回图里检查：它增强了哪段体验，引入什么资源，消耗什么资源，和哪些系统产生关系。这样设计与经营就能围绕同一个产品讨论。

English

A simulation example makes this collaboration concrete. Design proposes harvesting, building houses and functional buildings. Operations asks where the harvest goes, how storage is constrained, what upgrades consume and whether accumulated resources still lead to meaningful goals.

Together these questions form an economy. Production needs uses; consumption needs value; upgrades need a sense of progress; unlocks need anticipation. Ordinary currency, premium currency, materials and reputation each require a clear behavioral purpose.

Time and money must be considered together. How long does a non-paying player need to reach a goal? What experience does a payer receive? Does payment buy decoration, convenience, choice, content or speed? How do both paths coexist?

Operating input means examining the source of demand, intelligible value, sustainable content and the effect on earlier player investment. Draw the core loop, its extensions and resource flows. Return each proposed feature to that map and examine the experience, resources and system relationships it changes.

2.4 运营与技术：需求、埋点、工具与发布

一个商业目标要经过产品设计才能变成技术任务。先说你希望哪一群玩家的什么行为发生改变，再讨论需要新系统、新内容、配置改动还是仅仅改变展示。

我经历过一次跨品类的讨论：看到休闲产品的续命功能表现不错，有人希望在一个偏重度产品里加入类似机制。代码可能写得出来，但还要判断失败体验、资源结构和付费动机是否匹配。跨品类看到的好办法，需要先找到它在本产品里的成立条件。

技术协作也包括提前准备数据。来源、版本、进入活动、完成动作、购买、领取奖励，要按明确口径记录。某个按钮被点击，不等于奖励已成功到账；发起支付，也不等于付款成功。只记录界面动作，很可能看不见真正的失败。

需求还要包含可运维性。哪些参数可以配置？配置生效范围是什么？能否暂停活动？出现错误时怎样恢复？没有这些能力，运营就会在每次调整时依赖代码发布，后续节奏被开发排期锁住。

我更愿意在排期前讨论取舍。必须完成的体验、必须保留的数据、必须通过的异常检查要分清；可以延期的装饰或扩展内容另排。等全部做完才宣布少了关键事件，是最昂贵的一种“补充需求”。

English

A commercial target needs product design before it becomes an engineering task. Start with the audience and behavior to change, then decide whether that requires a system, content, configuration or presentation change.

One cross-category discussion I encountered concerned borrowing a revive mechanic from a casual title for a heavier game. It may be implementable, but its failure experience, resource structure and payment motivation still need to fit. Cross-category ideas require explicit transfer conditions.

Instrumentation must be prepared early. Define source, version, event entry, completion, purchase and reward claim. A click does not prove successful reward delivery; a payment attempt does not prove payment completion. Interface actions alone can hide operational failure.

Requirements also need operability: configurable parameters, scope of changes, pause controls and recovery. Without these, every adjustment depends on a code release.

Discuss trade-offs before scheduling. Separate essential experience, essential measurement and essential exception checks from optional polish. Discovering a missing critical event after completion is an expensive form of added scope.

2.5 运营与市场：承诺、档期、素材与预算

市场把玩家带过来之前，运营要交代清楚我们需要什么样的人、为什么需要，以及产品准备接住他们的方式。年龄、性别、地区只是起点，使用场景、游戏偏好、进入动机和付费习惯还要继续展开。

同时反向检查产品：设备和性能准备好了吗？新手体验能否兑现素材？来源追踪和漏斗事件能否工作？新用户礼包是否能领取？老玩家会怎样感受这次活动？服务器和客服是否知道时间与规模？

这里的协调要做到细处，因为营销效率和产品效率不能分开看。一个素材把点击做得很好，却吸引了对本产品长期体验没有兴趣的人，会把成本留给后面的环节。另一个素材看起来没那么热闹，却可能更准确地找到了目标玩家。

出现问题时，把两边的数据接在一起。曝光、点击、商店到达、安装、首次打开、关键体验、留存、付款，需要按来源和版本看。若进入前就离开，原因和进入后离开不同；若留下但没有付费，也不能只凭一个数字断定商品出了问题。

具体分析的价值，在于把下一次测试变小、变明确。换一个承诺，改一个入口，修一个加载问题，检查一个付款失败场景。每轮都应该知道自己在验证哪一种解释。

English

Before marketing brings players in, operations should specify the required audience, why it matters and how the product will receive it. Age, gender and market are starting points; context, preferences, entry motives and payment behavior add substance.

Check readiness in return: devices and performance, consistency between creative and onboarding, source tracking, funnel events, offer delivery, existing-player treatment, server capacity and support coverage.

Marketing efficiency and product efficiency must be read together. Strong clicks can attract players with little interest in the long-term experience. Less spectacular creative may identify a more suitable audience.

Connect impressions, clicks, store visits, installs, first opens, key experiences, retention and payments by source and version. Departure before entry differs from departure after play. Retention without payment still requires investigation rather than an immediate verdict on offers.

Diagnosis should make the next test smaller and clearer: one promise, one entry point, one loading issue or one payment failure. Each cycle needs to state the explanation it is testing.

2.6 分歧、交接与升级：谁决定，谁负责

团队意见不同并不可怕，怕的是争论结束后没有一个可以检查的下一步。策划说体验会变好，市场说用户会更多，技术说时间不够，运营要把这些话放到同一张决定表上。

先明确目标和约束，再写出可选方案。每个方案需要什么工作，会牺牲什么，能够在什么时间看到哪些证据。由有对应权限的人做决定，承担执行的人确认交付，后续有人跟进结果。

交接也要有可用的信息。一个任务写着“完成”，后面的人还可能不知道版本、配置、适用地区、未解决异常或如何回滚。交付状态至少要说明：交了什么，在哪里，谁检查了，剩什么，下一步谁接。

测试规模也不能用一个固定人数包打天下。少量玩家可能帮助发现明显的理解和流程问题；判断小幅留存或付费差异，需要根据事件发生率、波动、分流方式和观察窗口决定样本。小规模测试的经验有价值，但一个历史人数不能成为所有实验的充分条件。

选一项正在跨团队推进的需求，用下面的表做一次交接：

English

Disagreement becomes useful when it produces a testable next step. Put design expectations, marketing expectations and engineering constraints into the same decision record.

Define the goal and constraints, then options. Record required work, trade-offs, timing and evidence. The appropriate decision owner decides; delivery owners confirm the work; someone follows the outcome.

A “complete” label is insufficient for handover. The next person needs version, configuration, markets, unresolved exceptions and recovery instructions. Record what was delivered, where, who checked it, what remains and who takes it next.

Test size also depends on the question. A small group can expose obvious understanding and flow failures. Small retention or payment differences require sample planning based on event rates, variability, allocation and observation windows. A historical sample count is not a universal guarantee.

Use the handover table for a current task. A field answered with “we will decide later” identifies a collaboration risk that deserves attention now.

第03章 游戏生命周期：每个阶段做不同的决定

Chapter 03 · The Game Lifecycle: Different Decisions at Different Stages

开场 / Opening

我喜欢用做菜来讲项目阶段。立项时选的是要做什么菜、给谁吃、用什么材料；研发时把它做出来；上线时决定怎样端到人面前；长线运营则要让人愿意再来。

这几个阶段做的事不同，判断依据也不同。一个立项提案不能靠画面好看放行，一个已经上线的成熟产品也不能每周按照新项目的思路推倒重来。

我的工作里，新项目和经典产品再次商业化占了很大一部分。它们让我特别在意一个问题：今天做的这件事，适不适合产品当前的阶段？很多时候，方案本身有价值，放错时间就变成了负担。

English

I like using cooking to explain product stages. Greenlighting chooses what to make, for whom and with which ingredients. Development creates it. Launch decides how to serve it. Long-term operations gives people a reason to return.

Each stage requires different work and evidence. An attractive visual does not greenlight a business, and an established live title should not be rebuilt from first principles every week.

New projects and renewed commercialization of established titles have occupied much of my work. They make one question essential: does this intervention fit the product’s current stage? A valuable idea introduced at the wrong time can become a burden.

3.1 立项：竞品、市场、团队与回报假设

立项既可能从团队提案开始，也可能从老板的方向开始。起点不同，最终都要回答同样的问题：我们准备为哪一群用户提供什么，他们为什么会选择我们，团队能否在需要的时间里做出来。

研究竞品时，我会同时看产品机制、用户体验、内容与经营结构。排行榜上的位置只是结果入口。它可能来自长期积累、发行条件、品牌、内容供给或系统深度。想学习它，需要找到结果背后的成立条件。

还要把现在看见的市场，放到自己真正上线的时间里。一个需要较长制作周期的项目，面对的是未来的竞争环境。我会要求团队往前看六到十八个月，具体强调的是前瞻意识；具体跨度要与项目周期匹配。

做休闲产品研究时，我会把几款同类产品玩透，拆它们的循环、反馈、系统与商业化，再去判断自己的产品组合。把几种看起来有效的功能放在一起，仍然需要验证它们是否形成一致的体验。

立项文件至少要把用户、体验、市场、差异、团队、周期、成本、回报假设和主要风险放到一起。收入模型要能反推：需要多少活跃玩家，什么付费结构，多少持续内容，怎样的发行投入。算不清的地方，是下一轮研究或测试的任务。

English

Greenlighting may begin with a team proposal or a leadership direction. Both must answer the same questions: which audience, what experience, why they would choose it, and whether the team can deliver in time.

Competitor work should examine mechanisms, experience, content and commercial structure. A chart position is an entry point into an explanation. It may depend on accumulated audience, publishing conditions, brand, content supply or system depth. Find those conditions before transferring the approach.

Consider the market at your actual release date. My six-to-eighteen-month perspective expresses the need to look ahead; the horizon must fit the project.

In casual-game research, I play several comparable titles deeply, then analyze loops, feedback, systems and commercialization. Combining effective-looking features still requires testing for a coherent experience.

A proposal needs audience, experience, market, differentiation, team, timing, cost, return assumptions and risks. Work backward from the commercial model into active players, payment structure, ongoing content and publishing investment. Unresolved assumptions become research or test tasks.

3.2 MVP、焦点小组与内部测试

MVP不是把全部功能缩小一遍。它应该优先回答当前最可能让项目成立或失败的问题。核心反馈是否有吸引力，操作是否能理解，内容组合是否连贯，某个系统有没有明显缺陷，都可以成为一个具体测试题。

焦点小组可以帮助理解玩家怎么想、怎么描述体验；观察实际操作可以看见他在哪里犹豫、误解或退出。两类信息互补：玩家的解释提供线索，真实行为告诉你线索落在哪一步。

内部测试能发现功能、配置、性能与协作问题。内部同事熟悉产品，往往跳过新玩家会遇到的困难，因此还需要让没有背景知识的人进来体验。不要把“熟练的人能玩”当成“新用户能懂”。

测试前写下问题，规定要观察的行为和结束后要做的决定。若你同时改了入口、玩法、奖励和付费，又换了受众，结果发生变化时就很难知道哪一步起了作用。

我会把MVP、焦点小组、内测、用户测试、Soft Launch和后续调优连成验证路径。实际项目不一定按同一个顺序排，但每一轮都要知道减少了什么不确定性。做出东西是一个进度，回答了关键问题又是另一个进度。

English

An MVP should answer the question most likely to determine whether a project works. Attractive core feedback, understandable controls, coherent content and a critical system defect are different test questions.

Focus groups reveal how players think and describe an experience. Observed play reveals hesitation, misunderstanding and exit points. Explanations provide clues; behavior locates them within the journey.

Internal testing identifies functionality, configuration, performance and coordination problems. Colleagues know the product and can bypass difficulties that new players encounter. External players without prior knowledge are therefore important.

State the question, behavior to observe and subsequent decision before testing. Changing entry, gameplay, rewards, payment and audience together makes attribution difficult.

My operating flow connects MVPs, focus groups, internal and user tests, soft launch and iteration. The exact order can differ, but each round should reduce a named uncertainty. Producing an artifact and answering a critical question are separate forms of progress.

3.3 Soft Launch：用什么证据放行

Soft Launch在这里指有限范围、接近真实发行条件的上线测试。它可以限定地区、渠道、受众或规模，目的是看产品在更真实的环境里怎样运行。它与封闭内测不是同一个概念。

选择测试范围时，要知道什么结论可以带走。一个地区的成本和付费体验，不能自动代表另一个地区；自有社区里熟悉品牌的用户，不能自动代表冷启动渠道。它们都能提供价值，但需要标明条件。

放行门槛也不能只留一个“留存达到多少”。应同时看体验、稳定性、留存、商业化、获取成本、内容供给和团队修复能力。缺哪一块，对下一阶段的风险就要有明确解释。

门槛在测试前写，调整原因在测试后写。否则很容易每次结果出来，再找一个最漂亮的数字说明应该继续。指标低了可以迭代，但迭代要有机制解释、资源和期限；不是把测试无限延长就算坚持。

AI生成的Demo也适用这个要求。素材测试的点击表现可以验证某种表达的吸引力；它不能单独证明玩法留存或付费。将广告测试、真实体验和商业测试分别记录，才能知道现在究竟到了哪一步。

English

Here, soft launch means a limited release under conditions closer to actual distribution. It may restrict market, channel, audience or scale. It is distinct from a closed internal test.

Understand which conclusions transfer. Costs and payment behavior in one market do not automatically describe another. An owned community familiar with the brand does not automatically represent a cold acquisition audience. Label the conditions under which evidence was collected.

Release criteria should consider experience, stability, retention, monetization, acquisition cost, content supply and the team’s ability to repair issues. A single retention threshold cannot represent all of them.

Write criteria before the test and reasons for changes afterward. Weak results may justify iteration when there is a plausible mechanism, capacity and a deadline. An indefinitely extended test is not a decision.

An AI demo needs the same discipline. Creative clicks test the appeal of a promise; they do not establish gameplay retention or payment. Record creative, experience and commercial tests separately so maturity remains visible.

3.4 上线清单与发行时间窗口

上线需要一张检查表，也需要一张发行计划。检查表判断产品是否准备好；发行计划安排在什么时间，以什么规模，通过什么入口把玩家带来。

上线清单里，名字、包标识、包体、加载、离线策略、推送、IAP、宣传素材和数据模型都要考虑。具体项目还要按设备、市场、商业模式和实际功能把这些项展开。清单上有一行“支付完成”，并不能替代一次真实的支付验证。

English

Launch needs both a readiness checklist and a publishing plan. The checklist establishes preparedness; the plan coordinates when, at what scale and through which entry points players will arrive.

A launch checklist needs to consider names, package identifiers, size, loading, offline strategy, push, IAP, promotional assets and operating models. Expand these according to actual devices, markets, business model and functionality. A checked “payments” row does not replace payment verification.

The publishing plan connects release windows, stores, media, channels, creative, community and support. A delayed module requires examining its effect on the external promise, not simply moving an internal date.

Each acceptance should point to something traceable: version, configuration, observed behavior, event, image or record. AI can help prepare the checklist. Acceptance still depends on the product and its evidence.

3.5 成熟产品交接、调优与再次商业化

接手老产品，先知道它为什么长成现在的样子。以前的团队怎样判断，哪些系统之间有依赖，哪些配置还在工作，收入和玩家在哪些阶段发生过变化。看不见历史，最容易把必要的结构当成累赘。

我会把现状分成玩家、数据、产品、技术和组织几个面，先找到可信的基线。再区分能通过配置调整的事、需要新功能的事和动到底层的事。每一种改动用掉的资源和带来的风险都不同。

我遇到过一个很实际的取舍：当付费玩家规模不大，而老系统新增CRM需要较多开发时间时，先让专业VIP客服提供服务，可能更符合当时的经营约束。这是特定情况下的选择，不是说CRM没有价值。关键在于投入什么时候能转成有效服务。

成熟产品还要管理新旧体验。补商品、调节奏、做活动、改善反馈，都应该尊重玩家已有的目标与投入。收入上去了，却让最稳定的玩家失去回来的理由，这个改动还没有完成经营验收。

再次商业化，需要找到可释放的价值：还没有被好好服务的用户，没有被表达清楚的体验，缺少合适消费场景的资源，或现有团队无法持续承接的工作。对照基线逐项验证，效果才可以复制，而不只是记成一次救盘故事。

English

Begin a handover by understanding why the product became what it is: earlier decisions, dependencies, working configurations and changes in revenue and players. Without history, necessary structures can look like clutter.

Establish a trustworthy baseline across players, data, product, technology and organization. Separate configuration changes, new features and foundational changes; each has different cost and risk.

I have encountered a practical trade-off: when a small payer population and an older system make a new CRM expensive to implement, professional VIP support may provide effective service sooner. This is conditional judgment, not a rejection of CRM. The question is when investment turns into useful service.

New offers, cadence, events and feedback must respect established player goals and investment. A revenue increase that removes the reason loyal players return has not completed operating acceptance.

Renewed commercialization identifies unrealized value in underserved players, poorly expressed experiences, resources without valuable uses or work the existing team cannot sustain. Validate against the baseline to turn recovery into a repeatable practice.

3.6 生命周期转段、停损与产品收尾

一个阶段结束时，最重要的产出是下一步的决定。继续、调整、缩小范围、暂缓或停止，都需要说明依据、资源和责任人。

我会把“有问题”再拆一层：是体验机制不成立，还是实现有缺陷？是用户不匹配，还是渠道没找对？是经济深度不足，还是样本和观察窗口不够？不同的原因，对应不同的修复成本。

有时调整很值得做，有时需要承认当前资源无法完成它。已经投入的时间和钱要进入复盘，却不能独自证明下一笔投入值得。下一步仍要看未来能得到的证据与价值。

下面把产品收尾作为团队需要准备的经营阶段来讨论。它包括停止新增承诺、厘清已有玩家权益与服务、按适用要求安排通知、数据和支付处理、保存必要记录以及责任交接。具体操作应按实际产品和市场确认，不从任何一款游戏的经验直接套用。

Chapter 04 · Connecting Player Counts, Revenue and Profit

开场 / Opening

运营面对的经营问题，最后常常会落到几个看起来很简单的数字：多少人在玩，多少人付费，每个人贡献多少，投入多久能够收回来。真正困难的是让这些数字属于同一套事实。

我把收入拆成DAU、付费率和ARPPU，再继续追问新玩家、老玩家、商品与经济。这样拆的价值，在于把一个结果变成团队可以操作的输入。

海外扩充版把这套模型放到更前面，并把每一个口径讲清楚。后面不管讨论Arcade、Sims、Casino还是消费AI，都可以回到同一张经营表。产品不同，参数会变；数字之间的关系需要先一致。

English

Operating questions often resolve into deceptively simple numbers: how many people play, how many pay, what each contributes and when investment is recovered. The difficult part is making those numbers describe the same reality.

I decompose revenue into DAU, payer conversion and ARPPU, then examines new players, existing players, offers and economy. Decomposition turns an outcome into actionable inputs.

This edition brings the model forward and clarifies its definitions. Arcade, simulation, casino and consumer AI can return to a common operating worksheet. Parameters change by product; the relationships must first be consistent.

4.1 DAU模型：新增、留存、回流与流失

先定义“活跃”。打开客户端、完成核心动作或达到某种有效使用条件，会得到不同的集合。选定口径之后，把日期、时区、身份去重和事件规则一起固定。

当日活跃可以按互斥的玩家类别拆开：首次活跃的新玩家、符合连续或近期活跃定义的老玩家，以及超过规定间隔后回来的玩家。回流间隔要事先写清楚，这样一个人不会既被放进老玩家，又被放进回流。

DAU = 首次活跃的新玩家 + 持续/近期活跃的老玩家 + 按既定间隔定义的回流玩家。

安装数不一定等于新活跃。有人安装后没有打开，有人重装，有人换设备。渠道统计、商店统计和产品身份还可能使用不同的单位。先把安装、首次打开、首次有效活跃分开，再连接它们。

> 教学示例，非真实项目数据 / Illustrative example, not project results

项目 / Item数量 / Count
当日新增安装 / New installs10,000
满足首次活跃定义的独立新玩家 / Unique new active players7,000
持续/近期活跃的老玩家 / Continuing/recently active players26,000
按事先规则识别的回流玩家 / Reactivated players2,000
当日DAU，三类互斥 / DAU, with mutually exclusive groups35,000

分析未来DAU时，还要把不同日期进入的玩家分群。今天来的玩家以后是否回来，决定新增怎样累积成稳定的人数。不要把不同安装日的D1、D7混成同一条留存曲线，也不要把同一个用户跨日活跃相加后当独立人数。

流失也需要一个窗口。某人今天没来，不一定已经离开产品。选定没有活跃多久才算流失，再与回流使用一致的分类规则，才能做连续比较。

English

Define activity first. Opening the client, completing a core action and meeting a meaningful-use threshold produce different sets. Fix date, timezone, identity deduplication and event rules together.

Daily activity can be decomposed into mutually exclusive groups: first-time active players, continuing/recently active players, and players returning after a predefined interval. Define that interval so the same person cannot be both continuing and reactivated.

DAU = first-time active players + continuing/recently active players + reactivated players under the stated rule.

Installs are not necessarily new active players. Some installers never open; some reinstall or change devices. Store, channel and product systems may count different units. Keep installs, first opens and first qualifying activity distinct before connecting them.

The teaching example totals 35,000 active players, not 38,000: only 7,000 of the 10,000 installs qualify as new active players, and the three active groups are mutually exclusive.

Forecast DAU by acquisition cohort and its return behavior. Do not combine D1 and D7 from different cohort definitions or add daily activity counts and call the sum unique users. Churn also needs a window; absence today alone does not establish departure.

4.2 收入模型：DAU、付费率、ARPPU与广告收入

若本节讨论的是当日玩家付款，并且付款用户属于同一活跃身份范围，就可以这样拆：

当日玩家付款 = 当日活跃玩家数 × 当日付费率 × 当日ARPPU。

这里的付费率，是当日独立付款玩家占当日活跃玩家的比例；ARPPU，是该范围内玩家当日付款总额除以当日独立付款玩家数。不要把月付费率、累计ARPPU和当日DAU乘在一起。

再加上对应时期、范围和金额口径的广告收入，才能讨论这个范围的综合变现。若ARPU已经包含内购与广告，再额外加一次广告收入，就会重复计算。

同口径的综合金额 = 内购付款金额 + 广告收入金额 + 其他实际存在的商业模式金额。

要特别注意Web付款、订阅和实体产品。付款可能不经过游戏客户端，交易也可能对应不同的交付与确认时点。先把身份、时期和金额类型接清楚，再决定是否放进同一张表；不能因为都叫“收入”，就直接相加。

接下来继续追问商品、需求和经济循环。付费率改变，可能来自价值理解、时机、入口、价格或付款失败减少；ARPPU改变，可能来自商品组合、购买频次与用户结构。模型帮你定位下一步要查什么，不会自动给出唯一原因。

English

For daily player payments, when payers belong to the same active identity scope:

Daily player payments = DAU × daily payer conversion × daily ARPPU.

Daily payer conversion is unique daily payers divided by daily active players. Daily ARPPU is their payment total divided by unique daily payers. Do not multiply daily DAU by monthly conversion or cumulative ARPPU.

Add advertising only when period, population and amount definitions match. If ARPU already includes IAP and advertising, adding ads again double-counts them.

Combined amount on a consistent basis = IAP payment amount + advertising amount + amounts from other actual business models.

Web purchases, subscriptions and physical products require particular care. Payment may happen outside the client and relate to different delivery or recognition timing. Align identity, period and amount type before aggregation.

Continue into questions about offers, demand and economic circulation. Payer conversion can change through perceived value, timing, access, price or fewer checkout failures. ARPPU can change through offer mix, frequency or audience composition. The model identifies the next investigation; it does not establish a unique cause.

4.3 PCU、DAU、安装与付费用户的口径

PCU是某个观察时期内的峰值同时在线，不是当天独立活跃总人数。集中活动可能把在线峰值拉高，却没有带来同等程度的独立玩家增加。做活动前先确定你要提高哪一个。

DAU当日满足规则的独立玩家 / Unique qualifying daily players跨日求和当独立用户 / Daily sums treated as unique users

同一个付费玩家可以一天买三次。ARPPU计算的是他作为一个付款玩家的金额；每笔订单的平均金额则是另一个指标。商品分析需要后者，玩家价值分析往往需要前者。

每份报告至少标日期窗口、时区、地区、平台、版本、身份单位和金额口径。自动化会让报表传得更快，所以这些定义最好跟着数据走，留在导出和分析结果里。

English

PCU is peak simultaneous activity in a specified window, not unique activity across a day. An event may concentrate existing players and raise concurrency without a similar increase in DAU. Define the intended result before designing it.

A payer can buy three times in one day. ARPPU measures the amount per unique payer; average order value measures the amount per transaction. Both are useful for different questions.

Every report should carry its window, timezone, market, platform, version, identity unit and amount definition. Automation increases distribution speed, making portable definitions more important.

4.4 流水、净收入、毛利、利润与现金流

讨论“赚了多少”之前，先知道说的是哪一行。玩家支付的金额、平台可结算金额、公司会计收入和实际到账现金，需要分别对齐。手续费、分成、退款和其他扣减，又可能在不同的系统与时间里出现。

经营分析可以先做一张桥接表，显示某一统一时期内从付款到可用金额，再到投入与剩余。成本如何归集、何时确认，要与公司的具体口径保持一致。

English

Before discussing earnings, identify the row: player payment, platform-settlement amount, accounting revenue or cash received. Fees, shares, refunds and deductions can appear in different systems and periods.

Use a bridge from payment to available amount, then spending and remainder on a consistent period. Cost allocation and recognition must match the company’s definitions.

The example’s $3,000 is not $3,000 of profit. Its $80 remainder is not company net profit: it omits other corporate costs, taxes, currency effects, refunds, recognition differences and settlement timing.

Cash also needs a timeline. Acquisition spending may occur before platform settlement. Recorded revenue does not establish available cash. An expansion decision therefore needs both unit economics and funding capacity.

4.5 LTV、CAC、回收期与分群

LTV先说明你观察了多久、用什么金额。七天实际累计金额、三十天实际累计金额和预测长期价值，不能放在一列里不做标记。广告与内购要按相同玩家范围归属，采用收入或贡献价值也要从头到尾一致。

截至第N天的实测每用户价值 = 该分群截至第N天的累计同口径金额 ÷ 该分群初始独立用户数。

观察时点要定义清楚。如果“七天”指D0至D6，就按这七个自然观察日统计；如果使用从安装起的滚动168小时，就保持相同的规则。没有成熟到目标窗口的分群，不能用缺几天的数据和成熟分群直接比较。

CAC也要说明买的是谁。按安装算的是每安装成本，按首次活跃算的是每新活跃成本，按首付算的是每首付用户成本。把安装成本拿去和付款用户的LTV相比，会把分母悄悄换掉。

回收期是累计可用于回收的金额，在一致的成本口径下覆盖对应投入的时间。若分群尚未观察到回收，就写尚未回收；预测会在哪一天回收，另列假设。ROAS通常比较广告相关的回报金额与广告花费，也不能独自证明整体项目利润。

分群再按地区、渠道、素材承诺、版本和进入时间拆开。大盘平均数可能把两类相反的用户盖住。找到了哪一群玩家真正留下、付费并能覆盖投入，才有条件讨论扩大哪一段发行。

English

LTV requires a horizon and amount basis. Seven-day realized value, thirty-day realized value and predicted lifetime value need separate labels. Allocate IAP and advertising consistently to the same cohort, and keep revenue or contribution-value definitions consistent.

Realized value per user through day N = cumulative cohort amount on the stated basis ÷ original unique cohort users.

Specify whether seven days means D0–D6 or a rolling 168 hours. Immature cohorts cannot be compared directly with mature cohorts using incomplete windows.

CAC also needs a denominator: installs, new active users or first-time payers. Comparing cost per install with LTV per payer silently changes the population.

Payback is the time when cumulative recoverable value covers the corresponding investment on a consistent basis. An unrecouped cohort remains unrecouped; a forecast recovery date is a separate assumption. ROAS compares an advertising-related return amount with ad spending and does not, by itself, establish project profit.

Segment by market, channel, creative promise, version and entry date. Averages can conceal opposing populations. Identify who returns, pays and covers the relevant investment before deciding which distribution to expand.

4.6 把经营目标拆成可操作变量

假设团队希望下一个月表现更好，先把要求翻译成一个明确口径的结果。然后问：主要准备改善人数、付费率、付费频次、商品组合、广告收益、成本，还是几项共同作用？

每一个输入再落到玩家行为与团队交付。提高首次活跃，需要检查流量匹配、进入成本和新手体验；提高回访，需要检查目标、反馈、内容与关系；提高付款，需要检查价值、需求、时机和支付完成。动作落下去以后，还要检查其他输入是否被影响。

这套模型有价值，正因为它让产品、市场和团队都能进入同一张表。一个指标的变化可以提出假设，再用实验、实际体验和分群数据验证。

AI读这张表时，也必须保留定义。输入至少带上数据来源、时间窗口、金额类型和分母；输出要区分数据观察、解释假设和建议动作。让模型算得快，然后检查它算的是不是同一件事。

把“数据看起来不错”改成一个能复核的判断，后面的发行和经营决定才会更稳。

English

Translate a request for better next-month performance into a precisely defined outcome. Then decide which inputs to change: audience, payer conversion, purchase frequency, offer mix, ad yield, cost or a combination.

Connect each input to player behavior and delivery. First activity depends on audience fit, entry friction and onboarding. Return depends on goals, feedback, content and relationships. Payment depends on value, demand, timing and completion. Check effects on other inputs after the action.

The model brings product, marketing and teams into one worksheet. A metric change suggests a hypothesis to verify through experiments, play and cohort evidence.

AI needs the same definitions. Provide source, window, amount type and denominator; separate observations, explanatory hypotheses and proposed actions in the output. Faster calculation is useful only when it calculates the intended object.

Replace “the numbers look good” with a decision someone else can reproduce.
