# GGC Reply-First / 10,000 Executive Coverage SOP v1
Updated: 2026-10-10
Owner: Joyce / GGC

## 0. Priority inversion fix

**Human replies from real principal-side buyers or project owners outrank all new sourcing and all peripheral vendor / media / program communications.**

Mandatory execution order:
1. P0: Inbound buyer/project-owner messages requesting collaboration, sharing operating problems, introducing their principal, asking for a proposal/BP, mentioning budget/KPI/timing, or offering a meeting.
2. P1: Existing live buyer opportunity, proposal, meeting, procurement, decision blocker, and owner follow-up.
3. P2: Net-new untouched company census, identity matching, 3–5-executive graph, legitimate routes and approval-ready queues.
4. P3: Ecosystem / suppliers / creators / agencies / platform integrations / press / awards / unrelated administration. P3 is not a buyer KPI and must not displace P0–P2.

**Never equate UNREAD with low priority. Never equate IMPORTANT with real customer. Never equate human reply with qualified million-RMB buyer.** Read the actual content and whole thread.

## 1. Two different universes / two different CAPs

### Layer N: Chinese-speaking game decision-maker NETWORK, minimum 10,000 unique people

- Goal: build and pursue legitimate, relevant outreach to **at least 10,000 deduplicated real core executives/operators**, not 10,000 guessed email addresses.
- At 3–5 named decision-makers per company, this entails roughly **2,000–3,334 canonical company decision units**; this is a planning identity, not a verified market-size claim.
- Company Master scans Chinese-speaking / Chinese-led game developers and product owners across all genres. Core categories (Casino, Arcade, Sims, AI-native) are sector priority and tags, not a universal census exclusion.
- Tier N (network): actual responsible game-company leaders and budget/strategy/product owners; no million-year-contract presumption.
- Tier A (annual sales buyer): evidence of money, externalizable operating need, timing, buying process and accountable decision maker. Only Tier A enters million-RMB annual-contract forecast.
- Tier T (transformation): mature service / co-dev groups with verifiable move toward owned IP/self-publishing; research and review separately.
- Third-party marketing/KOL/UA agencies, media, events, investment funds, consultants, outsourcing vendors without principal-side interest are **not** counted toward the 10k principal network.
- FA/investor contacts remain in GGC-FA separate pipeline.

Never claim 10k as already sourced, approved, sent or in two-way conversation. Report these as separate cumulative totals:
**Unique Sourced Persons | Verified Current Roles | Verified Routes | Joyce-Approved First Touch | Confirmed First Touch SENT | Human Replies | Qualified Need | Meetings | Proposals | Signed | Cash.**

### Old CAP warning
The legacy 1,500–3,000 raw accounts / 150–350 sales-qualified accounts was an *annual-buyer scenario*, not a cap on the total game-executive relationship universe. It must not freeze broader sourcing. Million-contract qualification remains strict, applied only to the actual buyer forecast.

## 2. Reply Desk Service Levels

- **Scan** at least every 2 hours during supported run windows; do not claim actual 24/7 handling.
- P0 high-intent human buyer reply: report and address at the next run, aim for same business day, urgent meeting or deadline first.
- An actual commercial question / buyer signal must never wait behind third-party tasks or speculative research.
- Before response: retrieve full thread, prior SENT, actual sender identity, owner/direct contact, existing STOP and promises, existing proposals and price commitments.
- Draft concise role-specific acknowledgment; recognize **exactly what the other party said**, give one relevant next action, ask at most 1–2 decision-relevant questions.
- If they ask for a specific deck/price/meeting, answer that request (or specify verified blocker), not a new generic introduction.
- Never shotgun a diagnostic questionnaire or unsolicited price list at the first human reply.
- If already responded, classify ENGAGED_WAITING_INBOUND and do not resend or demand another answer.
- If connector fails: **check SENT by message ID / recipient / thread before retry**; after repeated errors, mark SEND_FAILED, provide ready-to-send reply and notify Joyce. No "sent" claim without read-back.

## 3. Reply classification by commercial signal

| Priority | Signal | Examples | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0-Now | Buyer urgency | Budget, KPI, price, procurement, proposal, meeting slots, start date | Address directly; confirm concrete next step and owner |
| P0-High | Product owner business need | Product problem, release, self-publishing, commercial bottleneck | Recognize specific need; ask minimal fit question / short call |
| P0-Intro | Introduction to decision maker | "负责人联系你", "加微信", handoff to GM | Give exact contact route, track handoff; no unfounded qualified sale |
| P1-Open | Genuine but unqualified contact | Earlier-stage external publishing, transformation | Reply respectfully; keep in network/partner or incubation, not million pipeline |
| HOLD | Explicit no need / not fit / later | "暂时不需要", "后面再聊", sender asks no more | Stop proactive sequence; save reason and reopen only by new meaningful trigger / Joyce approval |
| P3 | Third-party or non-buyer | Media, events, vendor, paid survey, platform | Defer; separate queue; never buyer conversion |
| Exclude | Auto reply, support ticket, OOO, bounce | "Forwarded internally" helpdesk, machine reply | No commercial reply/meeting count |

**No actual reply can be marked 'handled' simply because another generic outbound mail was sent.**

## 4. Customer-first daily production budget

Before any new outreach wave:
- Review outstanding P0 / P1 queues and resolve or surface blocking actions.
- Reserve the majority of BD analysis/work for **net-new core companies and core people**, not third-party activity.
- A 9am/9pm schedule is not a substitute for real-time reply routing; hourly condition-watch should identify outstanding P0.
- New-company outbound requires Joyce approval of company + recipient + message batch and current send gate.

Once P0/P1 are cleared:
- **Research → Verify Company → Verify 3–5 Core People → Route Verify → Dedupe / STOP → Approval Queue → First Touch → Thread Detection → Reply Qualification → Meeting / Scope / Cash**
- No address guessing and no blind mass contact.
- No automatic alternate-person rotation after no reply; company-level No-Reply Hold remains unless re-entry conditions are met.
- Banned companies: Yoozoo/游族 and NetDragon/网龙, including associated entities.
- Company rejections and opt-outs override all channels and agents.

## 5. Capacity model: how to reach 10,000

Do not confuse research speed with message delivery capacity.

- 10k distinct contacts / 180 calendar days ≈ 56 legitimate first touches/day.
- 10k / 90 calendar days ≈ 112/day.
- 10k / 60 calendar days ≈ 167/day.
- These are **arithmetic capacity scenarios**, not promised deliverability; total turnaround is constrained by user approvals, route quality, sender reputation, inbox/channel throughput, team resources and recipient relevance.
- Operate source ingestion independently from outreach: aim to identify 100–300 net-new company candidates per research batch where sources permit; map 3–5 actual decision makers for eligible priorities.
- Preserve a reserve of at least 2× approved wave size in contact-ready uncontacted executive records.
- Never loosen relevance, impersonate owners, reuse bounced email, or claim 10k outreach while only producing database records.

### Acquisition sources
- Developer/publisher identifiers across Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam, TapTap and product rankings.
- Industry-specific events / speaker / exhibitor directories; Chinese domestic and international conferences.
- Company sites, official team pages, verified public executives, hiring signals, announcements, public filings.
- Referral introductions and Joyce's consented professional network.
- Credible business databases when available and lawfully accessible.
- No scraped private contact details or addresses invented from naming patterns; observe platform and local data rules.

## 6. Technical pipeline / deduplication

Entity model:
- COMPANY: canonical parent/entity, domains, owned IP/products, sector tags, scale evidence, geography, exclusion, stop state.
- PERSON: real full name, current role, company FK, source URL, verified date, legitimate contact route, route confidence.
- THREAD: Gmail thread_id, direction, timestamp, sender, recipients, latest human inbound, latest confirmed outbound, meaningful signal, response due, label.
- APPROVAL: batch ID, company IDs, person IDs, draft hashes, approved timestamp/scope.
- OPPORTUNITY: company+person+product, priority, stage, amount/range where verified, buyer roles, next action, next due date.
- OUTREACH LEDGER: unique first-touch company/person, channel, sent evidence, bounce/suppression, and human reply.
- CONVERSION: stage numerator/denominator in clearly dated cohorts.

Deduplicate on canonical company/parent plus actual person identity; same-company aliases and shared email domains are not new contacts. Distinguish delivered vs sent, replies vs automated responses, meetings booked vs held.

A minimal useful daily report includes:
- **Outstanding P0 buyer replies** and count older than 24h
- P0 reply handling attempted / confirmed sent / blocked
- Human buyer replies handled / waiting, potential deal value
- Net-new source companies, verified executive names, verified legitimate routes
- Contact-ready and Joyce-approval pending batches
- Unique new persons actually first-touched this day / cumulative toward 10k
- Buyer responses and every stage conversion
- Hard bounces, duplicates, company STOP breaches
- One primary funnel break and next repair action

## 7. Route/volume safety gates

Quality before scale:
- Block send to unmatched entity, company No-Reply Hold, STOP, banned group or bad route.
- Pause any source after repeated hard bounces; review global failures against <3% aspirational guardrail.
- Ensure approved content is company- and role-specific, not template spray.
- Check whether last activity was a human reply; if yes route to P0, not new cold touch.
- Never send multi-party third-party messages merely to increase activity count.
- No new paid tools, scraped lead purchases or paid email infrastructure without prior cost and Joyce authorization.

## 8. Two independent outcomes

**Network Outcome:** 10k relevant verified executives eventually first-contacted through approved, legitimate channels, with actual response metrics.

**Commercial Outcome:** qualified principal-side buyer conversations → meetings → approved scope → signed engagement → money received.

Network volume cannot substitute for commercial progress. Commercial gate cannot prevent building the wider relevant network.
