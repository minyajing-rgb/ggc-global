# GGC BD Workspace

Updated: 2026-10-03

This directory contains GGC business-development positioning, pitch templates, account examples and operating materials.

## Mandatory workflow before drafting a pitch

1. Read [Joyce PR / BD Positioning Skill](../skills/joyce-pr-bd-positioning/SKILL.md).
2. Set the correct Joyce positioning, audience level, relevant proof and claim state.
3. Use [GGC Annual Client Development](../skills/ggc-annual-client-development/SKILL.md) for account qualification, executive mapping, route strategy and outreach controls.
4. Use [GGC Positioning to Close](../skills/ggc-positioning-to-close/SKILL.md) for offer, buyer, qualification, close and delivery design.
5. Check current Gmail/CRM/contact history and authorization before any external action.

## Positioning rule

Joyce is positioned as a **global multi-category interactive-entertainment operator and operating-system architect**, not as a generic consultant, report seller or isolated functional specialist.

A good pitch must connect:

> target signal → operating tension → one relevant Joyce proof → buyer value → proportionate next step

The differentiator is repeated cross-category operating judgment: identifying the variables that control results and connecting product, data, commercialization, global publishing and organization into an executable system.

## Legacy-template warning

Files such as `GGC_Master_Pitches_v1.md`, `GGC_Master_Intro_v1.md` and older pitch-email examples are historical working assets. They are **not automatically approved copy**.

Before reuse:
- replace generic biography-first openings with target-specific business relevance;
- re-check every metric, ranking, client/result claim and product status;
- do not combine outcomes from different projects;
- do not present current products/prototypes as proven market traction;
- do not use “growth” as a generic identity or filler;
- do not reduce Joyce's seniority to “consultant” merely to avoid claim review;
- record which proof was used and which stronger claims were deliberately held back.

## Required output for each new pitch

- account thesis;
- recipient role and angle;
- verified why-now signal;
- final pitch/message;
- proof used and evidence state;
- claims withheld;
- CTA and commercial next step;
- send/hold status under current outreach rules.
