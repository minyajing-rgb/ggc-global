5. Check current Gmail/CRM/contact history and authorization before any external action.

## Positioning rule

Joyce is positioned as a **global multi-category interactive-entertainment operator, Category Operating Architect and operating-system architect**, not as a generic consultant, report seller or isolated functional specialist.

A good pitch must connect:

> target signal → category/operating tension → controlling variables → one relevant Joyce proof → buyer value → appropriate commercial structure → proportionate next step

The differentiator is repeated cross-category operating judgment: identifying the variables that control results and connecting product, data, commercialization, global publishing and organization into an executable system.

## Commercial architecture rule

Do not automatically sell every opportunity as advisory hours.

For each qualified opportunity, decide whether it is:
- a bounded assessment or decision sprint;
- a milestone operating project;
- an annual embedded partnership;
- a co-build with performance/equity participation;
- or an asset/IP/product that GGC should own.

Ownership-linked structures require explicit baseline, attribution, data access, decision rights, implementation responsibility, IP and governance. Do not accept outcome accountability without the access and authority required to influence the outcome.

## Legacy-template warning

Files such as `GGC_Master_Pitches_v1.md`, `GGC_Master_Intro_v1.md` and older pitch-email examples are historical working assets. They are **not automatically approved copy**.

Before reuse:
- replace generic biography-first openings with target-specific business relevance;
- re-check every metric, ranking, client/result claim and product status;
- do not combine outcomes from different projects;
- do not present current products/prototypes as proven market traction;
- do not use “growth” as a generic identity or filler;
- do not reduce Joyce's seniority to “consultant” merely to avoid claim review;
- record which proof was used and which stronger claims were deliberately held back;
- decide whether the opportunity should produce cash fees, a longer operating mandate, participation, IP or ownership.

## Required output for each new pitch

- account thesis;
- recipient role and angle;
- verified why-now signal;
- category/operating tension and controlling variables;
- recommended Joyce/GGC operating role;
- recommended commercial and value-capture structure;
- final pitch/message;
- proof used and evidence state;
- claims withheld;
- CTA and commercial next step;
- send/hold status under current outreach rules.
