# Paid-Demand Gates

Use this reference to set evidence standards and produce consistent product decisions. These are GGC operating defaults, not universal market benchmarks.

## Evidence Ladder

| Level | Evidence | What it proves | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | Impressions, video views, visits | The offer can earn attention | Need, price acceptance, or demand |
| L1 | Likes, comments, survey interest, waitlist | The idea creates curiosity | A buyer will take purchase risk |
| L2 | Pricing clicks, checkout starts, quote requests, qualified buying calls | The buyer is considering action | Money or budget has been committed |
| L3 | Payment, deposit, card-backed paid trial, paid pilot, budget-backed signed commitment | A real buyer accepts commercial risk | The product delivers and retains value |
| L4 | Successful delivery, activation, outcome acceptance | The promised value can be delivered | Repeatability or healthy economics |
| L5 | Repeat use/payment, referrals, acceptable margin and support load | The product may deserve wider distribution | Unlimited scale or permanent advantage |

Use the highest level supported by auditable evidence. Do not upgrade an ambiguous signal to make a project pass.

## Stage Gates

| Gate | Minimum evidence | Permitted next step |
|---|---|---|
| Idea to test | Defined buyer, pain, offer, price, CTA, source, budget cap, kill rule | Create transaction test |
| Test to manual delivery | L3 commitment | Fulfill manually or run paid pilot |
| Manual delivery to MVP | Repeated L3 plus early L4 evidence | Build only the repeated bottleneck |
| MVP to public release | Reliable L4, manageable refunds/support, early repeat behavior | Broaden distribution carefully |
| Release to scale | L5 and workable contribution economics | Increase acquisition and automation |

Passing a gate authorizes only the next smallest stage.

## Default Test Thresholds

### Consumer and SaaS

- Prefer at least 3 non-affiliated paid commitments before an MVP build.
- Park or fundamentally reposition after 1,000 qualified visits with zero checkout starts.
- If checkout starts but no payments occur, test trust, payment friction, price framing, and promise before rebuilding the product.
- Count refunds and chargebacks against paid evidence.

### High-Ticket B2B

- Prefer at least 2 qualified buying conversations and 1 paid pilot, deposit, or budget-backed signed commitment before substantial build work.
- Park or redefine after contacting 30 qualified decision-makers with zero genuine need replies.
- Require the signer or sponsor to identify budget, decision authority, scope, and timing.
- An unpaid generic LOI, introduction, or positive meeting is L1 or L2, not L3.

### Game and Entertainment Products

- Prefer at least 5 non-affiliated paid founder-pack, preorder, deposit, or paid-beta commitments before expanding beyond a testable prototype.
- A single budget-backed publisher/platform commitment can substitute only when paired with playable-prototype or delivery evidence.
- Creative click-through, followers, wishlists, and Discord joins are useful L0-L2 signals but do not alone authorize full production.
- Validate refunds, play/engagement quality, and willingness to return before scaling production.

### Universal Kill Rule

Park after 3 materially different, honestly distributed variants produce no L3 commitment. A variant is material only when it changes a primary variable such as buyer, problem, offer, price, proof, channel, or CTA.

## Purchase-Intent Test Selection

Choose the strongest feasible commitment without pretending the full product exists:

| Situation | Preferred test |
|---|---|
| Simple consumer product | Working preorder or founder-price checkout |
| Software needing manual work | Paid concierge plan or card-backed paid beta |
| Expensive B2B solution | Paid diagnostic, paid pilot, or deposit-backed scope |
| Long procurement cycle | Signed commitment naming budget, authority, scope, and date |
| Game concept | Refundable founder deposit, paid alpha/beta, or preorder |
| Publisher/platform discussion | Budget-backed commitment plus prototype milestone |

## Decision Quality Checks

Before finalizing a memo, confirm:

- Traffic or outreach was reasonably qualified.
- The buyer saw an actual price.
- The CTA could complete or initiate a real transaction.
- Commitments came from non-affiliated buyers.
- Payments, refunds, and cancellations are netted honestly.
- The promise and delivery method were disclosed accurately.
- Only one main variable changes in the next iteration.
- The decision does not rely on vanity metrics.
