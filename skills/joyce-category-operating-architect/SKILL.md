## 1. Canonical commercial identity

### 1.1 Primary concept

**Category Operating Architect**

Chinese:

> **品类经营架构师**

Expanded Chinese:

> **全球游戏与互动娱乐多品类操盘手、品类经营系统架构师**

English:

> **Global multi-category operator and Category Operating Architect**

### 1.2 Definition

A Category Operating Architect can enter a market or product category that is still fragmented, noisy or poorly standardized and:

1. identify the variables that actually control player/user and commercial outcomes;
2. find the value pool, profit pool and winning loop;
3. connect product, economy, data, content, LiveOps, monetization, distribution and organization;
4. turn judgment into requirements, cadence, ownership, metrics and stop/scale rules;
5. create a system that can be repeated by a team rather than remaining personal intuition;
6. transfer the underlying system into adjacent categories without mechanically copying surface features.

### 1.3 Core commercial proposition

Joyce does not sell only “operations advice.” Her highest-value proposition is:

> **Identify the category's controlling variables and build the operating system required to compete for category leadership.**

Strong private first-person version:

> **我有一套可以跨品类迁移、反复做出全球第一或全球头部结果的经营系统：先找出真正控制结果的变量，再把产品、数据、商业化、发行和组织重新排成能执行、能复盘、能放大的结构。**

Public evidence-controlled version:

> **Joyce将跨多个全球游戏与互动娱乐品类的实战经验，沉淀为连接产品、数据、商业化、全球发行和组织执行的品类经营系统。**

Do not publicly use “repeatedly creates category leaders” unless the corresponding product/ranking matrix is approved for that context.

## 3. Category Leader Operating System

Use this sequence when assessing a new product, category or portfolio decision.

### 3.1 Category thesis

Define:
- user job / motivation;
- category boundaries and adjacent substitutes;
- why users enter, return, pay, share and leave;
- current leaders and their real advantage;
- underserved segment or structural opening;
- value pool and profit pool;
- market timing and why now;
- kill criteria.

### 3.2 Winning loop

Map:

```text
acquisition promise
→ first-session proof
→ core action
→ progression / collection / status
→ resource tension
→ content or social return reason
→ monetization moment
→ reactivation and long-term identity
```

Do not copy a competitor's surface features before the loop and user motivation are understood.

### 3.3 Product-economic architecture

Design:
- sources and sinks;
- pacing and unlocks;
- content supply;
- payer segmentation;
- offers, subscriptions, passes, VIP or other relevant models;
- retention-adjusted LTV;
- budget and payback logic;
- event and lifecycle portfolio.

### 3.4 Distribution and market sequence

Decide:
- first market and platform;
- soft-launch gates;
- organic/paid/channel assumptions;
- creative and message taxonomy;
- localization depth;
- launch, hold, expand or stop conditions.

### 3.5 Organization and governance

Define:
- decision owner;
- product/R&D/operations/publishing interfaces;
- WBS and milestone gates;
- metric dictionary and source of truth;
- review cadence;
- escalation path;
- stop/scale authority;
- budget and P&L ownership.

### 3.6 Systemization

A category thesis is incomplete until it becomes:
- a decision memo;
- a roadmap and backlog;
- an operating cadence;
- named owners;
- measurable gates;
- repeatable tools/templates;
- a learning log and version history.

## 4. Commercial offer architecture

Do not default every opportunity to a generic consulting retainer. Match the offer to the decision, access, accountability and value at stake.

| Offer | Buyer trigger | Core outputs | Typical commercial shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Category Thesis & Greenlight** | Entering a new category or deciding what to build | taxonomy, player jobs, value/profit pool, benchmark, thesis, kill criteria | fixed-fee assessment / decision sprint |
| **Product & Economy Architecture** | Product exists but loop, progression, payer or LTV ceiling is unclear | core loop, economy, FTUE, content/LiveOps, monetization, KPI and roadmap | sprint or milestone scope |
| **Global Launch Operating System** | R&D is ready; global publishing layer is weak | market sequence, launch gates, localization, UA economics, release and review cadence | project + launch milestones |
| **Stalled Product Reset** | Spend, content or team activity continues without convincing result | source audit, bottleneck, reset thesis, stop/scale plan, ownership and 30/60/90-day path | diagnostic + embedded recovery scope |
| **Portfolio / Founder Decision System** | Multiple projects compete for capital and leadership attention | portfolio map, greenlight/kill rules, P&L logic, review governance, resource allocation | executive advisory / annual partnership |
| **Embedded Operating Partner** | Company needs cross-functional leadership, not a report | decision rights, operating cadence, product/publishing/commercial coordination | annual principal-led partnership |
| **Co-build / Incubation** | Joyce/GGC contributes thesis, product system, execution and long-term value | shared product roadmap, IP, team, GTM, operating and value-capture structure | cash + milestone + performance/equity/JV |
| **Owned Product / Platform** | The best long-term use of the operating system is internal | owned IP, data, distribution, brand and recurring economics | GGC-owned or controlled asset |

### 4.1 Selection rule

Use a bounded service when:
- the question is narrow;
- access is limited;
- Joyce does not control implementation;
- attribution is weak;
- the client mainly needs a decision or audit.

Use an embedded or ownership-linked structure when:
- Joyce materially shapes category, product or portfolio value;
- the work spans multiple functions and quarters;
- implementation responsibility and access are real;
- baseline and attribution can be defined;
- the buyer grants suitable decision rights;
- long-term value materially exceeds the fee-only scope.

## 5. Ownership and value-capture architecture

### 5.1 Core principle

Joyce's next commercial stage is not merely creating more value. It is **retaining an appropriate share of the value she helps create**.

The structural risk is:

```text
Joyce identifies the category
→ builds the operating system
→ product/company value compounds
→ Joyce receives only salary, project fee or a small bonus
```

Therefore every material opportunity must explicitly ask:

> **Is this a service, an operating partnership, a co-build, or an asset Joyce/GGC should own?**

### 5.2 Value-capture ladder

From lowest to highest ownership:

1. fixed diagnostic / review fee;
2. milestone project fee;
3. prepaid retainer / annual partnership;
4. fee + measurable performance participation;
5. fee + advisory or operating equity;
6. co-build / JV / shared IP;
7. GGC-owned product, data, distribution or platform asset.

Do not automatically choose the highest rung. Choose the structure that matches responsibility, risk, capital, decision rights and evidence.

### 5.3 Ownership gate

Before proposing performance participation, equity or JV, define:
- baseline and metric definitions;
- Joyce/GGC's exact scope and authority;
- data access and audit rights;
- implementation owner;
- attribution window;
- vesting and milestone conditions;
- cash component and expense responsibility;
- IP ownership and license boundaries;
- dilution, buyback, transfer and exit terms;
- governance, dispute and termination mechanics;
- regulatory, tax and legal review.

No ownership-linked claim should rely on a vague promise that “Joyce will make it successful.”

### 5.4 Anti-patterns

Avoid:
- unlimited founder access inside a small advisory fee;
- giving away category thesis and full execution architecture during cold outreach;
- accepting performance accountability without decision rights or clean data;
- equity-only work for an unqualified team or weak asset;
- success fees without baseline, attribution and payment mechanics;
- building client-owned long-term assets when GGC should own or license the system;
- treating visibility, logos or “strategic partner” language as compensation.

## 6. Buyer-specific commercial narrative

### Founder / CEO

Lead with:
- category and profit pool;
- portfolio choice;
- product ceiling;
- organizational bottleneck;
- what should be stopped, scaled, rebuilt or owned.

Default angle:

> 我关注的不是给一个功能提建议，而是这个品类真正的价值池、产品上限和组织瓶颈在哪里，以及怎样把判断变成一套能持续运行的经营系统。

### GM / Studio / BU Head

Lead with:
- title ceiling;
- resource allocation;
- roadmap and release tension;
- LiveOps/economy/monetization integration;
- team ownership and execution cadence.

### Publishing / Commercial

Lead with:
- product readiness;
- market sequence;
- channel and cohort economics;
- partner model;
- what must be fixed before distribution is scaled.

### Investor / Board / Strategic Partner

Lead with:
- category signal quality;
- commercial diligence;
- whether the team can turn product signal into durable economics;
- post-investment operating ability;
- portfolio stop/scale and capital allocation.

### Media / Conference / Award

Lead with:
- why repeated cross-category results are not random;
- the operating system behind category leadership;
- what current industry transition makes the model relevant;
- concrete, evidence-controlled examples and audience takeaways.

## 10. Review gate

Before approving the result, ask:

1. Does this position Joyce as a Category Operating Architect rather than a generic consultant?
2. Is the buyer's category, product or portfolio decision explicit?
3. Have the controlling variables been identified rather than listing functions?
4. Does the proposed scope turn judgment into an executable operating system?
5. Is the commercial model appropriate to Joyce's responsibility and value contribution?
6. Should Joyce/GGC retain IP, equity, participation or ownership here?
7. Are decision rights, data access and implementation responsibility sufficient?
8. Is every exact ranking, outcome and role supported for this audience?
9. Has astrology/spiritual interpretation been removed as commercial evidence?
10. Did the copy avoid generic “growth,” empty superlatives and fake rarity probabilities?
11. Does it protect valuable methodology from being given away in cold outreach?
12. Is the next step clear and proportionate?

Return:
- **APPROVE** — capability, evidence, offer and value capture align;
- **REVISE** — direction is right but scope, proof or commercial structure needs repair;
- **HOLD** — material evidence, access, decision-right or value-capture issue is unresolved.
