# Harpagia / Ken Law Case Notes — Source Basis

## Status

This file records the user-provided article that motivated `ggc-ai-native-game-build-to-revenue`.
It is not an independently verified research memo.

## Case claims supplied in the article

The article states that:
- Ken Law previously worked at Amazon and Meta and had not personally coded for more than four years before returning to development.
- He first made a small card game, Big 2 Wild, in roughly six weeks.
- He later built Harpagia with AI-assisted development.
- Tools/workflow named in the article included ChatGPT, Cursor, React Native, Firebase, purchased assets, and freelancers for some screenshots/icons/video.
- Harpagia was free-to-download with in-app purchases.
- The article says first purchase was about USD 10, first-month sales about USD 150, second-month cumulative sales about USD 1,500.
- About six months after launch, a Reddit post generated both criticism and exposure.
- The article reports Ken's self-described monthly revenue moving from about USD 1,000 to USD 10,000 after that community exposure.
- By the September 2026 interview described in the article, it reports about USD 155,000 revenue over the prior year, about USD 13,000 average monthly revenue, over 50,000 iOS/Android downloads, no paid advertising, and about USD 500/month in ongoing asset/outsourcing/subscription costs.
- The article itself notes that these are interview/self-reported figures and not audited net profit.

## Operating lessons extracted

1. Use a deliberately small first project to rebuild shipping capability.
2. Put AI inside the production workflow, but keep product decisions and QA human-owned.
3. Break coding work into small manually testable tasks.
4. Ship to real users before the game feels finished.
5. First payment is stronger evidence than praise.
6. Classify criticism before editing the roadmap.
7. Distribution to an existing player community can be part of product development, not just marketing.
8. Monetization requires a real player value proposition and working purchase path.
9. Small-team economics improve when assets/services are bought rather than reinvented.
10. Iterate from real player behavior and payments, not from internal confidence.

## What this case does NOT prove

It does not, by itself, establish:
- typical revenue for solo AI game developers;
- causal attribution of revenue to AI;
- causal attribution of revenue increase solely to Reddit;
- profitability after all labor/tax/platform costs;
- universal IAP pricing;
- a repeatable acquisition benchmark.

Future GGC projects should treat the case as an operating example, not as a financial forecast.
