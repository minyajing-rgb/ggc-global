# Joyce PR & BD Positioning System

## 0. Mission and precedence

This is the upstream positioning skill for Joyce/GGC.

Use it before:
- media, award, conference and speaker applications;
- biographies, websites, decks, brochures and social profiles;
- founder/CEO introductions and executive referrals;
- BD emails, LinkedIn/WeChat messages, meeting openers and proposals;
- partnership, investor and ecosystem narratives;
- any copy that describes Joyce's track record, rarity or transferable capability.

This skill defines **what Joyce is, which proof matters, and how the story changes by audience**. It does not authorize sending, publishing, submitting or contacting anyone.

After positioning is set:
- use `../ggc-annual-client-development/SKILL.md` for principal-side account development;
- use `../ggc-positioning-to-close/SKILL.md` for offer, buyer, qualification and close design;
- use `../../pr/authority-engine/EVIDENCE_MAP.md` and `../../joyce/docs/PUBLIC-BUSINESS-MASTER.md` for public evidence boundaries;
- use `../../positioning/JOYCE_PERSONA.md` for approved identity and career context.

When this skill conflicts with an older generic introduction or pitch template, this skill controls the **positioning**, while the most recent evidence ledger controls the **public claim status**.

## 1. Canonical positioning

### 1.1 Core identity

**Joyce is a global multi-category interactive-entertainment operator and operating-system architect.**

Chinese default:

> **Joyce｜全球游戏与互动娱乐多品类操盘手、经营系统架构师。**

English default:

> **Joyce Mi | Global multi-category interactive entertainment operator and operating-system architect.**

### 1.2 What this means

Joyce is not valuable because she has merely worked on many products. Her differentiator is the repeated ability to:

1. enter a new category and identify the variables that actually control results;
2. connect user motivation, product design, economy, content, LiveOps, monetization, distribution, data and organization;
3. translate those variables into an operating model, roadmap, team rhythm and decision system;
4. move between 0→1, 1→10, stalled-product recovery and portfolio-level decisions;
5. transfer the operating engine across different game categories and into apps, pan-entertainment, DApp/Web3 and AI-native consumer/wellness products.

The core claim is therefore **repeatable cross-category operating judgment**, not broad curiosity and not a collection of unrelated experiences.

### 1.3 Full internal positioning

Joyce's first-party career account is that she has achieved global No.1 or globally leading outcomes across approximately **7–8 distinct game/product categories**, with additional operating experience in apps, pan-entertainment and DApp/Web3, and is now transferring that system into AI-native consumer and wellbeing products.

Use this full statement in:
- private founder/CEO conversations;
- qualified one-to-one BD;
- internal strategy, partner briefings and pitch preparation;
- materials where Joyce can personally explain the products, metrics, period and role.

For public PR, exact counts and “global No.1” wording must follow Section 4.

### 1.4 What Joyce is not

Do not flatten Joyce into:
- a generic consultant;
- a report/course seller;
- a single-genre specialist;
- a UA-only, LiveOps-only or data-only expert;
- an outsourced marketing vendor;
- an “AI influencer” without operating depth;
- a vague visionary whose claims are detached from products and teams.

Games and interactive entertainment remain the professional core. Apps, pan-entertainment, DApp/Web3 and AI-native wellbeing demonstrate transfer range and current building activity; they do not erase the game operating record.

## 2. The rare capability stack

Do not lead with an invented population probability or historical-person ranking. Explain the observable capability stack.

### 2.1 Repeated outcomes, not one hit

One successful product may result from timing, brand, distribution or team luck. Joyce's positioning is based on **repeated results across materially different categories, companies, teams and market conditions**.

### 2.2 Far transfer, not surface analogy

Joyce extracts the deep structure of a category and transfers it into another context. The transferable layer is:

```text
Human motivation
→ core loop and progression
→ economy and resource constraints
→ content supply and LiveOps rhythm
→ monetization and payer design
→ distribution and market sequencing
→ analytics and experimentation
→ organization, ownership and governance
→ P&L / portfolio decisions
```

A new category changes the parameters, content, audience and constraints. It does not require rebuilding Joyce's entire reasoning system from zero.

### 2.3 Full-stack operating integration

The differentiator is not isolated expertise. It is the ability to connect:
- category strategy and greenlight;
- product and R&D interface;
- data and experiments;
- economy and monetization;
- lifecycle and LiveOps;
- UA/creative and distribution economics;
- global publishing and localization;
- team design, WBS, ownership and operating cadence;
- founder, portfolio and commercial decisions.

### 2.4 Execution and organization

PR and BD copy must show that Joyce does not stop at analysis. Use evidence of:
- requirements and decision memos;
- operating models and KPI definitions;
- launch and LiveOps systems;
- cross-studio/cross-functional coordination;
- team, process and governance design;
- shipped products, operating surfaces and measurable product outcomes.

### 2.5 The strategic development arc

Joyce has already established the **multi-category operator** level.

The next public arc is:

> **individual judgment → codified operating system → repeatable teams → products/IP/equity → talent and capital multiplication**

PR should show this trajectory without prematurely claiming historical stature.

## 3. Narrative spine

Every substantial Joyce story should contain four layers.

### A. Past — proof of repeated operating depth

Use the most relevant subset of:
- Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga and Gameloft career experience;
- Zynga Product Manager / Lead Producer work across CityVille, FarmVille and Arcade;
- Gameloft global operations across Order & Chaos, Minion Rush, Thor 2 and other approved records;
- Chinese CityVille D1 retention +700 bps, scoped to that project;
- 10+ studios and 500+ cross-functional collaboration network, never “500 direct reports”;
- entrepreneurship, product building, publishing and executive operating roles;
- public book, speaking, mentoring and industry-method contributions.

Do not dump all credentials. Select the evidence that explains the audience's current problem.

### B. Pattern — why the outcomes repeat

State the operating engine:

> Joyce identifies the category's controlling variables, connects product, data, monetization, distribution and organization, and turns them into a repeatable decision and execution system.

This is the bridge between apparently different products.

### C. Present — what Joyce/GGC is building now

Depending on audience, use:
- GGC game incubation and global publishing work;
- category research and product/operating reviews;
- AI-native operating infrastructure;
- self-owned and co-built consumer products;
- current work across Casino, Arcade, Sims and AI-native wellbeing/companionship;
- tools and workflows that convert research into decisions and execution.

A live demo proves building activity, not automatically revenue, adoption or client success.

### D. Future — the larger ambition

The future is not “more consulting.” It is:
- building repeatable operating systems;
- incubating and co-building products;
- multiplying decision quality through teams and AI-native workflows;
- accumulating IP, products, equity, data, distribution and long-term partnerships;
- helping founders and studios make category, product, publishing and portfolio decisions with higher odds of success.

## 4. Claim and evidence governance

### 4.1 Evidence states

Every material claim must have one state:

| State | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Public verified/approved** | Public source or approved public master | Public PR, website, award, BD |
| **B — First-party career record** | Joyce's CV, profile, project record or direct account | Public with accurate attribution/context; private BD |
| **C — Private verified** | Internal record, contract, dashboard or approved confidential evidence | Private qualified BD; anonymized public use only with approval |
| **D — Pending reconciliation** | Claim exists but product, metric, period, source or role is incomplete | Internal only; do not publish as settled fact |
| **E — Current build/hypothesis** | Product, prototype, research or forward-looking thesis | Describe as current work, not achieved market outcome |

### 4.2 Exact ranking rule

Before writing “global No.1,” “category No.1,” “top-grossing,” “global leader” or an exact count such as “7–8 categories,” capture:

- product/project;
- category definition;
- platform;
- geography;
- metric/ranking definition;
- exact period;
- evidence/source;
- Joyce's title and responsibility;
- Joyce's specific decisions/actions;
- result attributable to the wider team versus Joyce's direct contribution;
- public/private approval state.

When the evidence is mixed, use a lower but still strong level of specificity:

1. **Exact:** “No.1 in [defined category/market/metric] during [period].”
2. **Scoped leading:** “A globally leading [category] product in [market/period].”
3. **Portfolio level:** “Repeatedly contributed to globally leading products across multiple categories.”
4. **Career level:** “Multi-category global game and interactive-entertainment operator.”

Do not erase the full career claim internally merely because every proof item is not yet consolidated. Do not publish the strongest exact wording without the corresponding context.

### 4.3 Non-negotiable boundaries

Never:
- combine rankings, profit, revenue, users or outcomes from different products into one implied case;
- state that Joyce directly managed 500+ people when the evidence is a cross-functional network;
- turn a researched company/product into a Joyce/GGC client;
- turn a live prototype into proven market traction;
- call a submitted application an award, finalist status or confirmed speaking engagement;
- invent a probability such as “one in 150 million” or a count of comparable people in human history;
- call Joyce “the only,” “the world's best,” an industry-standard setter or equivalent without direct evidence;
- publicly equate Joyce with a historical or business celebrity as if stature has already been proven;
- imply employer, customer or partner endorsement merely because Joyce worked with or for them.

### 4.4 Strong first-party language

For private, qualified BD, first-person career claims are permitted when they are Joyce's own account and she can substantiate them in discussion:

> “过去15+年，我跨7–8个游戏和数字消费品类做到全球第一或全球头部，并把同一套经营模型迁移到不同产品、团队和市场。”

For public use before the full ranking matrix is approved, default to:

> “过去15+年，Joyce跨多个游戏及互动娱乐品类，反复参与和推动全球头部产品建设，并把产品、数据、商业化、全球发行与组织执行连接成一套经营系统。”

## 6. BD pitch protocol

### 6.1 Required inputs

Before drafting, obtain:
- target company/product;
- recipient role;
- verified why-now signal and date;
- likely operating tension;
- one relevant Joyce proof item;
- proposed GGC scope or next step;
- prior relationship/contact state;
- claim status and confidentiality boundary.

### 6.2 Message architecture

Use this order:

1. **Target signal** — show why this account and why now.
2. **Operating tension** — identify the decision beneath the symptom.
3. **Relevant Joyce pattern** — one proof, not a CV dump.
4. **Buyer value** — what decision, operating system or outcome Joyce can help unlock.
5. **Small next step** — a specific conversation, assessment or comparison.

The default first-touch structure is:

```text
[Observed signal]
→ [specific operating question/tension]
→ [one relevant Joyce/GGC proof]
→ [why the fit may matter]
→ [low-friction next step]
```

### 6.3 Role-specific angle

- **Founder/CEO:** portfolio, category, profit pool, decision quality, organization and operating leverage.
- **GM/Studio/BU:** title ceiling, resource allocation, release/LiveOps, stop-scale and team ownership.
- **Publishing/Commercial:** market sequence, publishing layer, partner model and commercial readiness.
- **Product/Ops/LiveOps:** core loop, retention, payer/LTV, events, data and operating cadence.
- **Investor/Strategic:** thesis validation, commercial diligence, post-investment execution and portfolio signal.

Do not send the same copy to 3–5 executives in one account.

### 6.4 Copy rules

- Lead with the target's business reality, not flattery.
- Use one strongest relevant proof; keep the rest for the conversation.
- Do not use “growth” as a generic identity or filler. Use the exact business outcome: revenue, margin, retention, LTV, market expansion, product scale, operating leverage or portfolio value.
- Do not automatically request 30 minutes. Match the CTA to context: reply, 15–20 minute comparison, scoped review, referral or working session.
- Do not reveal the full methodology in a cold message.
- Do not promise outcomes Joyce/GGC cannot control.

### 6.5 Required output for a new BD pitch

Return:
1. account thesis;
2. recipient/role angle;
3. subject/opening options;
4. final message;
5. proof used and evidence state;
6. claims deliberately held back;
7. CTA and next commercial step.

## 7. PR, media and speaking protocol

### 7.1 PR story formula

A strong Joyce PR story is:

> **A repeated industry problem → Joyce's unusual cross-category pattern → concrete evidence → the operating system behind it → what she is building now → why it matters to the audience.**

PR is not a longer CV and not a list of company logos.

### 7.2 Media pitch formula

```text
Current industry tension
→ counterintuitive Joyce point of view
→ 2–3 evidence-backed examples
→ useful story/data/access offered
→ why this publication/audience now
```

### 7.3 Speaker pitch formula

```text
Audience decision/problem
→ what most teams get wrong
→ Joyce's repeated cross-category evidence
→ 3 concrete takeaways
→ recent work that makes the topic current
```

### 7.4 Award formula

```text
Scope and responsibility
→ challenge
→ Joyce's actions/decisions
→ measurable result
→ repeatability/system
→ wider contribution
```

Do not use the award application itself as evidence of recognition.

## 9. Internal archetype benchmarks

These are internal strategy analogies, not public equivalence claims.

- **叶茂中型维度:** a professional method that transfers across many categories.
- **段永平型维度:** judgment codified into organization, leaders, companies and capital allocation.
- **范蠡型维度:** the ability to read different rule systems, choose timing, enter/exit and rebuild value.

Use these archetypes to identify Joyce's next capability and asset-building priorities. Never write that Joyce “is the next” or already has the same historical stature.

## 10. Review gate

Before approving any PR or BD copy, answer:

1. What audience and decision is this for?
2. Does the first sentence position Joyce at the correct level?
3. Does it show repeated cross-category operating capability rather than a task list?
4. Is every exact metric/rank scoped by product, market, period and role?
5. Are first-party, private, public and forward-looking claims distinguishable?
6. Is the selected proof relevant to the recipient rather than merely impressive?
7. Does the copy connect Joyce's past record to current GGC work and the next step?
8. Did it avoid generic “growth” language, empty superlatives and probability claims?
9. Did it avoid turning prototypes, research or applications into achieved outcomes?
10. Is there a clear, proportionate CTA?

Return one status:
- **APPROVE** — positioning, evidence and audience fit are ready;
- **REVISE** — direction is right but wording/evidence needs repair;
- **HOLD** — a material claim or audience assumption is unresolved.

When revising, preserve Joyce's seniority and differentiator. Do not solve evidence risk by reducing her to “consultant,” “advisor” or a list of functional skills.
