## 1. Core ICP

Priority is **Chinese-speaking founders and game teams**, with **Mainland China first**, then Greater China / overseas Chinese-founded teams.

The ideal prospect is not simply “a game company.” It is a team with a proven product/business base but a clear capability gap when moving from one operating model to the next.

### A. Domestic-first teams going global
Typical profile:
- Previously focused on China / domestic market.
- Has proven R&D or commercial results.
- Now wants to expand overseas.
- Missing one or more of: global publishing, GTM, UA, monetization, LiveOps, localization, overseas organization, data/KPI system.

Best GGC path:
- **GGC Copilot**
- Global GTM / Publishing Readiness
- External COO / CMO support
- Global operating system build-out

### B. Overseas custom / co-development teams moving to own IP
Typical profile:
- Previously did overseas customization, co-development, OEM / project-based work.
- Already understands overseas clients/products.
- Now wants to create self-developed products and own the upside.
- Needs product selection, monetization, publishing, market validation, growth, or capital support.

Best GGC path:
- **Copilot + Product / GTM**
- Incubation if still pre-launch
- FA / capital connection if fundable
- Publishing if product reaches B / B+ readiness

### C. Pure R&D / CP teams that no longer want to rely on publishers
Typical profile:
- Historically developed games and handed them to external publishers.
- Wants to build an internal publishing / self-publishing capability.
- Founder does not want another publisher-led model.
- Needs to build overseas publishing team, UA, LiveOps, monetization, CRM, community, data stack and operational cadence.

Best GGC path:
- **Self-Publishing Build-out**
- GGC Copilot
- External COO / CMO
- Publishing organization design
- 90–180 day operating system build

### D. Proven founder-led studios with a “missing corner”
Typical profile:
- Product/R&D is strong but publishing weak.
- UA is strong but monetization / long-term LTV weak.
- Domestic execution strong but global weak.
- Overseas distribution exists but product depth / LiveOps weak.
- Multiple teams/subsidiaries exist but lack a unified growth/publishing system.

GGC pitch:
> You do not need a generic consultant. You need the missing operating layer that can move the company from a strong local/product business into a globally scalable one.

## 2. Commercial Size Filter

Priority prospects should have real capacity to buy a **high-value annual framework / retainer**, but pre-contact evidence and post-contact qualification are different stages.

### 2.1 Outreach Eligibility economics
For first-touch eligibility, exact private-company profit is not mandatory when it is not public.

Acceptable evidence is a credible combination of:
- durable high-grossing / high-margin owned products;
- multi-year live operation and sustained monetization;
- meaningful paid-UA activity over time;
- multiple mature owned titles;
- listed/audited parent or group economics;
- credible mid-market / vertical-leader portfolio scale;
- current overseas/self-publishing hiring or expansion;
- other verifiable cash-generation proxies consistent with purchasing a high-value service.

Classify:
- **Verified** — public/audited evidence is strong;
- **Strongly Inferred** — multiple credible operating signals support ability to pay;
- **Pending Evidence** — not enough evidence yet.

Only Verified or Strongly Inferred accounts may enter normal outbound. Pending Evidence stays in research unless Joyce explicitly approves.

### 2.2 Sales Qualification economics
For the **RMB 1M-class annual-client pipeline**, retain the stronger standard:
- company/core-product annual profit around **RMB 30M+**, OR
- equivalent strong current cash generation / confirmed budget;
- RMB 100M+ profit or clearly scaled cash generation remains highest priority.

Revenue, downloads, financing, employee count, or "looking for publishing" do **not** independently prove payment capacity.

Do not invent financials. Use observable signals and label uncertainty.

## 3. Decision-maker Priority

Contact in this order:
1. Founder / CEO / Co-founder
2. Group GM / Studio Head
3. Head of Overseas / Head of Publishing
4. COO / CMO / VP Growth
5. Product / Publishing business lead with budget influence

Chinese language is preferred for Chinese-speaking targets unless the company’s public working language is clearly English.

---

## 4. High-intent Buying Signals

Score up when the company shows one or more:
- Recruiting overseas publishing / UA / LiveOps / localization roles.
- Recently formed an overseas business unit.
- New self-developed game entering soft launch overseas.
- Previously publisher-dependent product now self-published.
- Founder publicly says they want to build own publishing capability.
- Recent financing intended for global expansion / self-developed IP.
- Domestic hit but weak overseas footprint.
- Strong downloads but weak monetization / grossing.
- Several studios/subsidiaries but fragmented publishing operations.
- New product line in Sims / Arcade / Casino / AI-native entertainment.
- Seeking co-publishing, overseas GTM, strategic capital, or operating partner.

## 7. Exclusion / Low Priority

Lower priority unless there is an exceptional signal:
- Pure outsourcing vendor with no own-product ambition.
- Very early hobby indie with no product/data/funding signal.
- Small CP / early studio without credible payment-capacity signals, even if it needs publishing. Exact RMB 30M+ profit is not required for first touch, but weak/no cash-generation evidence still excludes the account from annual-client sales.
- Teams seeking only cheap execution labor.
- Companies already fully mature in global publishing with no identifiable GGC “missing corner.”
- No clear decision-maker or commercial capacity.
