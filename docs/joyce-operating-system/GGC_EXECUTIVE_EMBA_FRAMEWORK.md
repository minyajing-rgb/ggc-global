# GGC Executive EMBA Framework

> Version: 2026-10-08  
> Audience: Founder / CEO / VP / SVP / BU Head / Studio Head / senior publishing and operations leaders.  
> Purpose: an executive-level system for understanding, evaluating, and operating global game businesses.

# Positioning

This is **not a junior operations course** and not a tutorial about isolated tactics.

It is an executive framework for:
- category judgment
- product and business model decisions
- evidence-based validation
- global publishing
- long-term operation
- organizational design
- capital efficiency
- AI-native operating leverage

The teaching rule is:

**Explain the judgment system publicly; reserve proprietary scoring, thresholds, templates, and project-specific execution logic for paid / internal work.**

# Capstone — Executive Operating Review

A capstone review should include:

## Part A. Business truth
- current product stage
- key evidence
- major uncertainty
- economic reality

## Part B. Strategic judgment
- where to play
- product thesis
- biggest risk
- resource choice

## Part C. Operating plan
- next milestone
- owner
- date
- evidence threshold

## Part D. Compounding
- what reusable asset will remain after the cycle?

# What Makes This Executive-Level

This framework is designed to move the conversation from:

- "Which tactic should I copy?"
to
- **"What decision are we making, what evidence supports it, and what system will compound after execution?"**

That is the intended Joyce / GGC level of discourse.
