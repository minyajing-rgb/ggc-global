# GGC Principles

> Version: 2026-10-08  
> Use: company principles, founder-facing positioning, internal product reviews, partner selection, executive sharing.

## Principle 01 — Globalize by Design

**全球化从产品定义开始，不从翻译开始。**

A global product considers market structure, user expectation, monetization, creative language, distribution, and operating rhythm before the product is "finished".

**Anti-pattern:** build locally, then ask publishing to "make it global".

---

## Principle 02 — Judgment Before Activity

**先判断，再行动。**

Being busy is not evidence of progress.

Before adding people, budget, features, content, or tools, define the decision that the work is supposed to improve.

**Executive test:** What will we know after this work that we do not know today?

---

## Principle 03 — Validation Before Scale

**先验证，再放大。**

Scale should follow evidence.

Evidence can come from:
- player behavior
- market response
- retention
- payer behavior
- unit economics
- repeatable operating signal

**Anti-pattern:** using budget to postpone a product truth.

---

## Principle 04 — One System: Product × Market × Business

**产品、市场、运营、商业化不是四摊事。**

A good product can fail because its market expression is wrong.  
A good campaign can fail because the product cannot retain.  
A strong retention loop can still fail if the economics do not work.

GGC reviews the system, not only the function.

---

## Principle 05 — Real Users Beat Internal Consensus

**真实用户信号，比内部共识更值钱。**

Leadership conviction matters, but conviction should be converted into testable hypotheses.

The goal is not to "prove the boss right".  
The goal is to learn fast enough to make the next decision better.

---

## Principle 06 — Cash Flow Is Product Truth

**商业结果不是最后一步。**

Revenue, payback, payer behavior, retention, and cost structure expose whether the product creates durable value.

This does not mean every early experiment must monetize immediately.  
It means the commercial hypothesis should exist early enough to test.

---

## Principle 07 — Same Principle, Different Organization

**原则可以复用，组织打法不能照抄。**

Large public company, scale-up, startup, and turnaround environments have different constraints.

What changes:
- decision rights
- team topology
- cadence
- risk tolerance
- capital efficiency
- communication cost

What remains:
- evidence
- accountability
- product truth
- operating economics

---

## Principle 08 — Build Once, Compound Further

**每次项目都要留下资产。**

A completed project should leave:
- better benchmarks
- cleaner data
- reusable templates
- proven decision rules
- stronger partner graph
- sharper category insight
- better tools

Otherwise, the next project starts from zero again.

---

## Principle 09 — Human Judgment, AI Leverage

**AI放大能力，不替代判断。**

AI can increase:
- research volume
- content variants
- analysis speed
- operating coverage
- workflow density

AI does not automatically improve:
- category selection
- player empathy
- business model quality
- decision accountability

The human remains responsible for the judgment layer.

---

## Principle 10 — Selected Partners > Generic Outsourcing

**关键能力必须由对的人承担。**

GGC prefers partners who have:
- domain depth
- survived multiple product / industry cycles
- evidence of delivery
- strong operating interfaces
- clear ownership boundaries

The goal is not to own everything.  
The goal is to assemble the strongest system around the important problem.

---

## Principle 11 — Stay in the Arena

**仍在一线，判断才不会过时。**

GGC continues to:
- research
- prototype
- publish
- operate
- test self-owned products
- work with selected external projects

This is a deliberate principle.

Insight should continuously meet reality.

---

## Principle 12 — Connect What Matters

**连接真正重要的东西。**

GGC's long-term lens is not only "more content" or "more automation".

Connect:
- people to people
- products to markets
- teams to evidence
- ideas to real users
- content to business
- experience to reusable capability

Brand-language candidate:

**GLOBALIZE · GAMIFY · CONNECT**
