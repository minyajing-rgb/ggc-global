## Long-term alliance / credential / award design
Suggested working names: `Global Game Operations Alliance` and `GGC Operations Lab` (names are concepts, not registered bodies).
Stage 0: independent practice group, open research notes and expert peer discussion.
Stage 1: training/workshops with truthful **course completion badges**, a public syllabus, assessment and issuer metadata; do not label these government, university or accredited qualifications.
Stage 2: optional independent competence assessment with documented rubric, reviewer independence, conflict-of-interest policy, appeal and revocation, verification directory, renewal terms, privacy and anti-pay-to-win protections. Call it a **GGC-issued private credential** only when these processes exist.
Stage 3: institutionally partnered recognition or awards only after examining legal jurisdiction, approval requirements, publicity rights, naming/trademark rules, award categories, jury governance, sponsor conflicts and regulatory restrictions. Joining another association does not authorize issuing awards under its name.

**Mainland China legal checkpoint (2026-08-01 onward):** the `社会组织评比表彰活动管理办法` (民发〔2026〕31号) superseded 2022 rules. Formal awards/recognition by registered social organizations are regulated; globally/nationally styled names, unapproved co-sponsored awards and related badges can raise restrictions. Classify business training, editorial lists and formal award activities separately. Get qualified legal review for the actual entity, location, marketing copy, titles and revenue model before launching.
Official source: https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=106676
