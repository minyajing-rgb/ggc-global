# Joyce Mi / GGC — Speaker Kit

Version: 2026-10-03-v3

## Speaker positioning

**Global multi-category game operator and operating-system architect | Global flagship games | Data/social virality and Asia method transfer | Cross-company operating systems | AI-native next chapter**

Joyce is best used for executive / practitioner audiences that want operational frameworks and concrete decision logic rather than high-level AI trend commentary.

## Preferred formats

- 20–30 min solo talk
- 30–45 min featured session
- 30 min fireside chat
- Executive roundtable
- Panel only when the topic and participant mix are strategically relevant

For new invitations, a solo session / fireside / featured interview has higher content value than a generic multi-person panel.

## Signature sessions

See [SIGNATURE_TALKS.md](SIGNATURE_TALKS.md) for full abstracts.

1. **From Global Hits to Operating Systems** — global flagship decisions, measurable systems and repeatable cross-company practice. PRIMARY authority talk.
2. **Zynga to Asia: Data, Social Virality, and Operating Disciplines** — what travelled from Zynga's DAU/revenue/experimental practice into Asian teams (first-party contribution; precise adoption claims need evidence).
3. **The Architecture of a Global Game Business** — product, analytics, game economy, monetization, organization, LiveOps and publishing.
4. **How Global Games Actually Win** — decision gates and launch systems.
5. **What Should We Build Next?** — category and portfolio judgment.
6. **AI Is Rebuilding the Game Business** — new tools built on the historic operating foundation; not Joyce's sole professional identity.

Each can be delivered in 15 / 30 / 45 minute variants.

## Audience fit

Best:
- game founders / CEOs / studio heads;
- publishing, product, LiveOps, monetization and analytics leaders;
- investors evaluating game / AI consumer opportunities;
- cloud / platform / AI ecosystems serving game companies;
- executive audiences exploring AI-native operating design.

## Organizer package

When replying to an organizer, send:
- 1 short bio;
- 1 primary talk + 2 alternates;
- 3 audience takeaways;
- portrait path;
- Joyce / GGC URL;
- selected speaking links from the newsroom;
- optional book reference when relevant.

For direct organizer or professional connection, use [CONTACT_CHANNELS.md](CONTACT_CHANNELS.md): WeChat / Telegram for suitable Chinese-language relationships, LinkedIn / Telegram for overseas contacts, and business / OpsGo content links when relevant. Contact options supplement the required application process; they do not turn a submission receipt into programme interest or acceptance.

Do not send the full service rate card unless the organizer is discussing a commercial workshop / advisory engagement.

## Invitation qualification

Before self-funding travel, confirm as many of the following as possible:
- solo / fireside / featured format rather than only generic panel;
- official speaker page;
- organizer photography / recording rights;
- official distribution or interview;
- VIP / executive networking access;
- relevant attendee / company mix;
- travel / hotel support where appropriate;
- ability to schedule business meetings around the event.

A stage appearance should create reusable authority assets or qualified executive relationships, not only an attendance photo.
