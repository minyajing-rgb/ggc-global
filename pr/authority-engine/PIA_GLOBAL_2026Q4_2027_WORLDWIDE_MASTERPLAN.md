## 一、按影响力层级，而不是“活动越多越好”

1. **Authority**：国际主办方独立 featured/keynote、官方 speaker profile + 视频回放；全球奖项决赛/获奖；专业媒体深访。
2. **Network**：全球游戏/AI高管、发行商、平台、CEO及投资人；产业协会的长期 mentor、委员会或专题组织者。
3. **Company PR**：GGC 经营系统与 AI-native 全球发行/研究产品；公司管理/创新/AI 生态奖项及国际专栏。
4. **Consumer Product**：Bunny/Astro/PLOT 各自单独展示已发布实物与产品的用户体验；SAGA Lab 研究/文化合作路线单独讲长期架构。
5. **China–Global bridge**：向海外主办方贡献中国头部开发者资源、全球成功方法论、地区项目复盘和跨境圆桌，争取官方合办而非免费“代招商”。

## 七、给主办方明确的价值交换

**Offer**: independently deliver a 20–40 min structured talk; real-world system redesign methods; global product and team leadership; China developer/founder ecosystem introductions subject to personal consent; case-study-based roundtable; future editorial content; international program co-design.

**Ask**: independent talk/featured fireside first; official speaker/executive profile; organizer social amplification; high-res event photos/session recording and reusable rights; thoughtful editorial interview or earned coverage consideration; complimentary VIP access/1:1 match-making; relevant founders/publishers/investors intros; potential travel/accommodation or honorarium, if policy permits; long-term city-to-city co-created programmes. No presumption of payment or sponsor status.

**Negotiation**: ask what the organizer can genuinely offer, what it expects in return, which team approves each item, and whether there is a no-fee, value-exchange pilot before anything commercial.
