## Extended bio — English

Joyce Mi is the founder of GGC — Global Game Copilot, a globally experienced flagship-game operator and operating-system architect whose distinguishing strength is personally leading game titles to flagship status, category leadership and commercially scalable execution. Her founder-provided career dossier records **five products built 0→1**, **dozens of titles scaled 1→10**, recurrent monthly profit outcomes in the tens of millions across mature titles, **six nine-figure project outcomes** in the historical account, and a company-level contribution of **more than RMB 1 billion in annual profit improvement**. These are aggregated career and portfolio-scale results across different settings, not the performance of one title or of today's GGC.

At Zynga, Joyce worked on CityVille, FarmVille and social/Arcade game franchises, integrating DAU and revenue forecasting, K-factor social sharing, user research, experimentation, retention, game economy and monetization. She helped transfer these early data-driven operating methods to Chinese and Asian game teams and social-platform ecosystems; these disciplines supported China and Asia's shift toward data-driven F2P commercialization and category-leadership decisions. At Gameloft, she helped build regional operating systems involving franchises such as Order & Chaos, Minion Rush and Thor 2, coordinating **10+ studios and a 500+ cross-functional collaboration network** around launches, LiveOps, financial and product decisions.

Joyce subsequently built development and publishing companies, combining team formation, category strategy, product portfolios, budget, publishing gates and substantial commercial improvements. That entrepreneurial history includes a game-development venture associated with a Beijing Stock Exchange listing and a publishing company that was acquired. She is the author of *Game Operations and Global Expansion* (China Machine Press, 2024) and has public industry-speaking records from 2019–2026. Today, at GGC, she applies this career-built methodology to international game businesses and AI-native consumer product systems.
