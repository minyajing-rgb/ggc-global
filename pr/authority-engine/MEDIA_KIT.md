Joyce Mi is Founder of GGC and a global game operator with 15+ years across games and product. Her career includes Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga and Gameloft. At GGC, her current focus includes AI-native game and consumer-product operating systems, global commercialization and product incubation.

### 1. AI-native games
- Why AI in games is moving beyond asset production.
- Product decision systems, LiveOps, monetization and publishing with AI.
- Where human review / accountability must remain explicit.
- How small teams can use AI without turning operating quality into prompt chaos.

### 2. Global game operations
- Soft launch: what to validate before scale.
- Retention, monetization and player lifecycle as one operating system.
- How global publishing differs from translation / localization.
- Why product, UA, LiveOps and data cannot be managed as isolated functions.

### 3. Category / product thesis
- How to evaluate Casino, Arcade and Sims opportunities.
- What a team should test in the first 3 seconds, 30 minutes and 7 days.
- How content burden, art burden and event burden change studio economics.
- What founders should kill early rather than scale late.
