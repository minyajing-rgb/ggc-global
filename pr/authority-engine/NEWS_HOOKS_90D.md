# Joyce / GGC — 90-Day News Hook Calendar

Version: 2026-10-01-v1  
Window: Q4 2026

The hooks below are designed to be **reportable ideas**, not company announcements. Each needs data, examples or a clearly bounded operator perspective before pitching.

## H1 — AI moved from asset generation to operating decisions

**Timing:** October  
**Headline thesis:** The important AI shift in games is no longer image / copy production; it is product review, experiment design, LiveOps, monetization and executive decision support.

Proof to package:
- anonymized workflow examples
- where human approval remains mandatory
- decision latency / rework reduction categories
- failure cases where more AI output made teams worse

Best targets: PocketGamer.biz, Game Developer, GamesBeat, VentureBeat AI, Naavik, Deconstructor of Fun, Tech in Asia.

## H2 — The “3 seconds / 30 minutes / Day 7” greenlight framework

**Timing:** October  
**Headline thesis:** Before a studio debates feature depth, it should be able to explain what the player understands in 3 seconds, unlocks in 30 minutes and is still nurturing after Day 7.

Proof to package:
- Sims / Arcade / Casino comparisons
- content burden and art burden
- small-team operating ceiling
- kill criteria

Best targets: Game Developer, MobileGamer.biz, PocketGamer.biz, Naavik, producer / design tracks.

## H3 — AI creative volume is outrunning retention architecture

**Timing:** October–November  
**Headline thesis:** Teams can now produce far more ad variants, but creative throughput does not repair weak D7/D30 retention or payer depth.

Proof to package:
- creative velocity vs retention examples
- Social Casino / hybrid format observations
- pre-launch retention gate
- UA stop / continue logic

Best targets: MobileGamer.biz, PocketGamer.biz, GamesBeat, AppsFlyer / Adjust / MAU ecosystem media.

## H4 — 2027 Global Game Operator Outlook

**Timing:** November  
**Headline thesis:** What founders should expect in 2027 across Casino, Arcade, Sims, LiveOps, UA economics, platform policy and AI-native team design.

Deliverable:
- 12–20 page GGC executive report
- 6 charts / decision tables
- 10 quotable predictions with confidence / evidence state
- one 30-minute signature talk

Best targets: broad games media, investors, conference producers.

## H5 — Small teams can ship bigger products, but content debt becomes the new bottleneck

**Timing:** November  
**Headline thesis:** AI lowers some production costs, but it can increase LiveOps, QA, content governance and decision complexity if the operating model is not redesigned.

Proof:
- team-size / content-burden matrix
- outsourcing / QA questions
- AI-assisted pipeline boundaries
- “output per head” without unsupported job-cut claims

Best targets: Game Developer, GamesBeat, Tech in Asia, e27, founder podcasts.

## H6 — Soft launch should be treated as a decision system, not a launch event

**Timing:** November–December  
**Headline thesis:** A useful soft launch has explicit stop / continue gates across retention, payer conversion, LTV, CPI, creative and market fit before spend increases.

Deliverable:
- GGC Soft Launch Gate template
- examples by category
- operator checklist
- 15-minute talk variant

Best targets: PocketGamer.biz, MobileGamer.biz, Naavik, PGC producers.

## H7 — China / Asia game studios do not mainly have a product problem; they have a global operating-layer problem

**Timing:** December  
**Headline thesis:** Strong development teams often reach the limit of distribution, operating rhythm, monetization and cross-market decision systems before they reach the limit of product quality.

Proof:
- public speaking observations from Wuhan / Xiamen / other industry rooms
- anonymized recurring founder questions
- global publishing operating map

Best targets: Tech in Asia, e27, White Whale, Chinese game / overseas media, Singapore executive audiences.

## H8 — What 2026 actually changed in game operations

**Timing:** mid / late December  
**Headline thesis:** Separate durable changes from temporary hype: AI production, creative testing, live-service design, D2C, platform policy and portfolio resource allocation.

Deliverable:
- “What changed / what did not / what to watch in 2027” table
- 5 operator lessons
- 5 falsifiable 2027 watch items

Best targets: year-end editorial roundups, podcasts, January conference producers.

## Use rule

A hook graduates to media pitch only when it has:
1. a one-sentence thesis;
2. at least two evidence points or clearly labeled operator observations;
3. a relevant audience;
4. one concrete takeaway the reader can use;
5. no unsupported aggregate-performance claims.
