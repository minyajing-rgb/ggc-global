## 4YFN27: Game operating systems

### From Global Hits to Operating Systems: What Founders Can Learn from Game Franchises

### 30-minute agenda

- 00:00–03:00 — Define the operating system: product choices, economics, people and decision rights.

- 03:00–09:00 — Historical flagship practice: separate a product decision from the operating infrastructure that sustains it.

- 09:00–16:00 — Map the studio system: greenlight, economy, analytics, LiveOps and international publishing.

- 16:00–22:00 — Founder exercise: identify one bottleneck, its accountable owner and a stop/go test.

- 22:00–25:00 — Translate the system to AI workflows without confusing prototypes with commercial outcomes.

- 25:00–30:00 — Questions and discussion.

### Three takeaways

1. Map product, commercial and organizational decisions into one operating system.

2. Choose a leading signal, accountable owner and decision threshold for a studio bottleneck.

3. Transfer mature game-operating practices to small teams without copying enterprise overhead.

## 4YFN27: Consumer AI

### From Play to Meaning: Building AI-Native Consumer Experiences People Return To

### 30-minute agenda

- 00:00–03:00 — The product question: useful return behavior, not a convincing first conversation.

- 03:00–09:00 — Map first value, repeat value and a voluntary return loop using the 3 seconds / 30 minutes / Day 7 lens.

- 09:00–15:00 — Bunny card-reading walkthrough: original content, user intention and a bounded reflective session.

- 15:00–21:00 — Design a seven-day cohort experiment and a paid-demand test; distinguish checkout starts from payments.

- 21:00–25:00 — Budget inference and editorial work; set memory controls and proceed/revise/stop criteria.

- 25:00–30:00 — Questions and discussion.

### Three takeaways

1. Translate a consumer need into first value, repeat value and a voluntary return loop.

2. Design a seven-day test with meaningful-return cohorts, quality checks and explicit decision thresholds.

3. Test willingness to pay separately from engagement while budgeting inference, content and user controls.

## Data Summit 2027

### From Answers to Decisions: Building AI Copilots with Human Accountability

### 30-minute agenda

- 00:00–03:00 — Why a plausible AI recommendation is not yet an executable business decision.

- 03:00–09:00 — Define a game-launch question and assemble relevant, attributable evidence.

- 09:00–15:00 — Compare alternatives, surface uncertainty and document unresolved assumptions.

- 15:00–21:00 — Audience exercise: inspect a sample recommendation for evidence, owner and approval boundary.

- 21:00–25:00 — Separate analysis, approval and execution; record what happened and what remains unverified.

- 25:00–30:00 — Questions and discussion.

### Three takeaways

1. Structure an AI recommendation around evidence, alternatives, uncertainty and an accountable owner.

2. Separate producing analysis from approving and executing a commercial decision.

3. Audit a proposed action for missing evidence and required human approval.
