# PIA execution SOP · source → opportunity → international authority

## 1. Source and prioritization (full coverage, paced execution)
Each Monday check all 74 records, not only the 10 most obvious events. Prioritize:
1) submission/correction deadlines ≤14d; 2) organizer's direct human asks; 3) accepted-program logistics; 4) high-authority zero-fee independent CFP; 5) company PR awards; 6) 2027 date/CFP watches. Work P0 before P1 before P2 only after deadline and benefit checks.

Score for human judgment: relevance to Joyce's career/roles & GGC brands (0–5), organizer stature/earned media (0–5), solo/keynote potential (0–5), networking/direct founder opportunity (0–5), paid cost/travel burden (−0…5). Never invent numerical venue rank. Critical deadline can temporarily override.

## 2. Lifecycle controlled states
- RESEARCH_OR_NEXT_WINDOW: official submission not yet open or organizer/eligibility needs checking
- READY_TO_QUALIFY: not submitted; make suitable theme, official route, selection / payout terms
- DRAFT_NOT_SUBMITTED: forms exist but no final receipt
- FORM_PARTIAL_NOT_SUBMITTED: data entered; final send incomplete
- NEEDS_RECEIPT_RECONCILIATION: user says submitted but category-specific receipt missing
- SUBMITTED_AWAITING_ORGANIZER: organizer official success or mail response confirming submission; not accepted
- CONTACTED_AWAITING_REPLY: person contacted; not a booked appearance
- HUMAN_REPLY_INTERNAL_ROUTING: substantive reply, internal routing; still no formal offer
- ACCEPTED_PROGRAM_NEGOTIATION: selected into a programme (e.g. WMF), attendance/payment/benefits not settled
- HOLD_ELIGIBILITY_OR_CLOSED: wrong rights/geo/closed/paid without permission
- ACCEPTED_CONFIRMED / PUBLISHED_CONFIRMED / WON_CONFIRMED: only when organizer's primary evidence exists. Complete with public proof/receipts in evidence log.

No state reset merely because a conversation switches between GPT, Codex or Work.

## 3. Email/CFP outreach
1. Check Gmail against existing thread; one organization + one route, dedupe.
2. For new accounts first present proposed company/organizer **names** for Joyce approval; do not spray emails.
3. Tailor each Pitch to media, keynote, company award or specific consumer product; do not use a generic self-promotional email.
4. When contacted, ask for:
   - solo / featured / keynote or fireside first; panel acceptable as entry where valuable;
   - official speaker profile & publicity, recording/photography, sharing rights, interview/editorial consideration;
   - VIP/developer/publisher/investor introductions, 1:1 meetings and co-curated China–global salon;
   - available speaker passes, travel/lodging/honorarium (if any) **without demanding payment as a condition of talking**;
   - named owner / programming timeline / next response date.
5. Offer globally tested game operating systems, Zynga→China methodology, Gameloft cross-studio systems, 2024 book, 2019–26 conference record, Chinese and global studio/founder access **subject to individual consent**, AI-native game + consumer work and a clear audience takeaway.
6. No follow-up after decline, hard bounce, or format mismatch; after no response, only if genuinely new value or organizer-stated response window; prefer 7–14 days or a milestone, not bulk blind follow-ups.
7. Submission success must have official receipt/success page; never infer from saved draft or email in SENT only.

## 4. Award vs. travel / media scope
- Free application: may submit when user-authorized and all eligibility facts present, without committing to attendance.
- Fee, deposits, flights/hotels, rights assignment, equity, work contracts: Joyce decision after exact cost and benefits.
- Signed declaration means only personally true facts; no fabricating rankings, period, client outcomes. Do not create unnecessary detective work when form doesn't require it.
- Speaker/award deliverable must be genuinely acceptable in English, concise, supported by existing LinkedIn/book/talk/materials.
- Separate personal Joyce awards, corporate GGC, Bunny/Astro/PLOT product applications and planned SAGA Lab research partner pitches.

## 5. Weekly retro process
At a fixed weekly recurrence:
- Sync from Gmail and published official pages where supported.
- Compare 74-row master by ID to previous snapshot; preserve untouched rows.
- Count only newly confirmed statuses; distinguish real progress, no response, deadline missed and pending Joyce approvals.
- Pick 5–10 actions with owner, due, source and next step.
- Review 3 root causes (wrong format/weak angle/late entry/repeat email/paid ROI/no staff).
- Make ≤3 tested changes to Pitch, channels, prioritization and follow-up intervals.
- Write `reviews/YYYY-Www.md` and update CSV + relevant open issues. If connectors not available at execution, report precisely which sources could not be checked, no invented progress.

## 6. Reporting statuses
🟩 RECEIVED/ACCEPTED with organizer proof only; 🟨 IN_FLIGHT with next check; 🟥 DEADLINE/BLOCKED; 🟦 NOT_STARTED/RESEARCH. No green for generic sent-only.
