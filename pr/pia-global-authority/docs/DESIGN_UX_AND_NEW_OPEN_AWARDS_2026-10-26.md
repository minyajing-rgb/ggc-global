**Design award narrative A - Chromatic Breathing (125 words):**
Chromatic Breathing transforms guided breathing into a clean, visual, touch-led digital ritual. Rather than relying on dense instruction, the interface uses smoothly changing color to represent breathing phases, helping users follow an intuitive inhale, hold and exhale sequence. Users can select patterns, personalize palettes, set their pace and inspect their session progress. The design addresses a familiar interface challenge: creating a low-friction moment of focus on a small screen without adding cognitive overload. The application is publicly available on the Apple App Store, with version history documenting its 2025 release and improvements. This nomination is for the quality of the user experience and interface design, not a clinical claim of efficacy or a claim of independently measured health outcomes.

**Design award narrative B - Bunny (97 words):**
Bunny Crystal Oracle is an original visual identity and interactive digital experience built around crystal-inspired cards and AI-assisted personal reflection. Its public web interface introduces a coherent illustrated world, themed prompts and an accessible path through short card-based interactions. The design challenge is to combine evocative creative content with comprehensible, user-directed interaction rather than rely on an undifferentiated AI chat window. The current public experience, including its original card collection and first-session pathways, can be independently inspected. Any award claim about measured user improvement, subscription success or platform-wide mobile distribution requires separate evidence.

**Concept UX answer - PLOT (105 words):**
PLOT explores how a relationship-reflection assistant could help people reason about ambiguous social experiences without imposing a deterministic judgment. Its concept experience is designed around a short, practical user journey: describe a real event in the user's own words, receive a structured interpretation that makes uncertainty explicit, and consider one actionable next step.
