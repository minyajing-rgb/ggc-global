## Statement 1 - The problem and why AI was appropriate

The operating problem Joyce set out to solve was not a lack of ideas or tools. Game and consumer-product teams were already surrounded by dashboards, research reports, creative assets and AI-generated output, yet important decisions were still fragmented across product, monetization, LiveOps, user acquisition, publishing and management. The same evidence was repeatedly re-collected, assumptions were not consistently challenged, and the speed of AI generation risked making weak decisions happen faster.

Joyce therefore treated AI as a decision-support layer rather than an autonomous decision-maker. The appropriate use case was to compress high-volume research, compare hypotheses, structure evidence, generate bounded alternatives and make expert operating knowledge reusable. Final commercial, product and public-facing decisions remain human-owned.

This distinction is central to the system she has built at GGC. AI is used where scale, synthesis and iteration matter; human operators retain responsibility where context, ethics, player impact, cultural nuance, commercial risk or unverifiable claims require judgment.

## Statement 2 - Joyce's personal contribution and actions

Joyce personally designed the operating logic that connects research to decision and decision to execution. Drawing on 15+ years across Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga, Gameloft and entrepreneurship, she translated senior game-operations judgment into repeatable AI-assisted workflows covering market research, product review, monetization, LiveOps, user acquisition, international publishing, content QA and executive decision support.

Her role was not simply to sponsor an AI project. She defined the questions each workflow must answer, the evidence required before a recommendation can move forward, the points where a human must review or approve, and the boundaries between public evidence, first-party records and unverified claims.

The current public GGC business system exposes 14 capability domains and 16 scoped services rather than presenting AI as an opaque universal solution. In parallel, Joyce has used the same operating logic across a multi-product portfolio in games, education, culture, wellness and science, turning the system into a working practice rather than a one-off demonstration.

## Statement 3 - Responsible AI safeguards and human accountability

The operating principle is simple: AI can accelerate judgment, but it cannot inherit accountability.

Joyce's workflows therefore separate generation from approval. Recommendations must retain their source context; first-party operating records are distinguished from independently verified evidence; application, PR and commercial claims are not promoted to a stronger status without an external receipt or source; sensitive or regulated areas require narrower scope and stronger human review.

The same discipline applies to product work. AI-generated options are treated as hypotheses to test, not facts to publish. Regional and cultural context is reviewed before global recommendations are reused. Automation is bounded by explicit decision rights rather than allowed to create irreversible outcomes by default.

This approach also addresses a common customer-experience risk: when teams optimise only for speed or efficiency, they can remove the human context needed to understand why a player or customer behaves a certain way. Joyce's model keeps evidence, user impact and human judgment in the loop so AI makes the organisation more capable without making responsibility ambiguous.

## Statement 4 - Outcomes and wider impact

The main outcome is a reusable operating capability rather than a single AI feature. GGC's current public business platform organises Joyce's work into 14 capability domains and 16 scoped services, creating a common language for decisions that previously sat in separate functions. The same operating model now spans a broader product portfolio and can be reused from research and product review through launch and ongoing operations.

Joyce has also carried this practice into industry education. In March 2026, White Whale / Baijing published the programme for a game-and-app international expansion event in Wuhan where Joyce presented on how AI is reshaping acquisition, product features, operations and team efficiency, alongside speakers from Tencent Cloud, NewsBreak Ads and AppMagic. Her book, industry writing and 2019-2026 speaking record extend the knowledge-transfer layer beyond GGC itself.

The contribution is intentionally practical: make AI useful where it is strong, create safeguards where it is weak, and leave final accountability with named human decision-makers.
