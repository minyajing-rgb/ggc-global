## Revised English narrative statements (review; adapt to exact official category question fields)

### 1. Original contribution to CX practice

My contribution has been to turn player-experience evidence into an operating architecture used to guide how games are designed, released, monetized and sustained. Across more than fifteen years, I have connected daily active users, cohort retention, revenue modelling, social/viral loops, research and experimentation with product economy, LiveOps, acquisition, publishing and accountable management decisions. My Product Manager / Lead Producer work at Zynga across CityVille, FarmVille and Arcade products gave me practical experience building and applying that model-led approach. I later worked to adapt and transfer operating methods learned at Zynga into teams in Asia. The distinctive contribution is not inventing a metric but building shared definitions, review rhythms and decision ownership so customer behaviour changes product choices rather than only appearing in a report.

### 2. Influence beyond one organization

My career spans Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga and Gameloft, followed by creating development and publishing businesses and advising other teams. At Gameloft, I worked on global franchises including Order & Chaos, Minion Rush and Thor 2, and designed regional analytics and operating approaches connecting forecasts, release planning, game economy, customer retention, monetization and LiveOps across more than ten studios and a 500-plus-person **cross-functional collaboration network** (not 500 direct reports). My wider career includes repeatedly establishing or rebuilding product review, team roles, workflows, launch gates, monetization and operating governance in different business and public-company project environments. Rather than presenting each title as a stand-alone achievement, I want to show what travelled between teams and organizations: measurable evidence, common decision rules and operational handoffs that could be implemented again.

### 3. Tangible changes and measurable results

A scoped project in my Zynga China career record achieved an improvement of 700 basis points — seven percentage points — in Day-1 player retention. That specific result illustrates how behavioural insight, experiments and execution can lead to a better player experience; it is not a portfolio-wide claim. My Gameloft work connected multiple development studios and a large operating network around lifecycle, economy, regional and commercial decisions. My entrepreneurial and advisory work subsequently applied those principles to launch readiness, cross-functional handoffs, turnaround and monetization decisions. For sensitive clients, I would supply permitted anonymized before/after examples showing who adopted an operating mechanism, what action changed, the period and what players or teams gained, rather than combining different companies' private financial metrics into a misleading total. I am compiling the relevant adoption artifacts and external references to support this history.

### 4. Sustained knowledge sharing and professional benefit

I have also codified and shared these methods outside my own companies. In 2024, China Machine Press published my book *游戏运营与出海实战：策略、方法与技巧* (*Game Operations and Global Expansion: Strategies, Methods, and Skills*; ISBN 978-7-111-75037-6), under my author byline 艾小米. My industry talks and organizer records span 2019–2026 and cover global game operations, user acquisition, commercialization, lifecycle, product decision systems and, more recently, AI-supported decision-making. I have contributed as a Tencent entrepreneurship-community mentor and through industry articles, workshops and advisory work. This sharing is not just visibility: the goal has been to enable others to build reusable operating capabilities. Claims that particular readers or advisory teams subsequently reached a global category #1 should be illustrated with attributable, permissioned client or reader testimony before being used as measured impact evidence.
