## Proposed statement 1 — Original contribution to CX practice
Joyce Mi's contribution is the translation of live-game product operations into a practical discipline of customer-experience decisions. Across global game operations, she has worked to link user behaviour, retention, content pacing, in-product economies, purchasing patterns and regional context to decisions that affect what customers actually experience—not merely to retrospective dashboards.

The core idea is an accountable operating loop: customer evidence → product hypothesis → controlled test → human go/no-go decision → measurable improvement or discontinuation. She has carried this approach into GGC Games, where consulting and AI-assisted operating tools aim to make experienced judgment reusable by teams. It helps leaders ask not only whether an experience drives engagement, but whether the experience benefits customers and makes economic and operational sense over time.

## Proposed statement 4 — Knowledge sharing and sustained contribution
Joyce has continued to teach and share operational methods beyond her own product teams. Her published book, professional articles, public speaking and Tencent entrepreneurship-community mentorship create channels through which founders and operating professionals can access the decision frameworks she developed over her career.
