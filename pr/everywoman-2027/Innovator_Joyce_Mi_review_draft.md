This is executable application code, not merely a slide or concept. Another evidence stream includes the GGC website and public consumer product ecosystem, with live GGC/OpsGo and SAGA1001 pages, alongside game research dashboards, project control views and automation/QA workflows. Joyce's technical approach solves a practical problem: small, expert-led teams must coordinate analysis, product decisions, production, growth and quality assurance without reproducing the overhead of a large organization. She treats domain expertise as executable operational knowledge: a Skill encodes the work, an Agent performs a bounded task, a web tool makes the output usable, an evidence gate checks claims, and a human owner approves actions and learns from results. This forms a repeatable path from **problem → Skill/Agent → data/content/code → quality gate → live user or business feedback → next iteration**.

Her experience makes this more than generic AI experimentation. At Gameloft, as Head of Asian Operations, she coordinated operating work across more than ten studios and a 500-plus cross-functional collaboration network, including global *Order & Chaos* and *Heroes of Order & Chaos* and *Minion Rush* in China. That history supplies the real operating judgment she now turns into machine-assisted repeatable tasks; it does not imply 500 direct reports.
