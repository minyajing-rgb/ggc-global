# GGC Strategic Media Partnership Menu

Version: 2026-10-07 v1.0

## Purpose

GGC works with credible game/App media in two clearly separated lanes:

1. **Independent editorial** — the outlet controls whether and how it reports; commercial cooperation never guarantees coverage.
2. **Commercial/media partnership** — agreed event, research, studio, distribution or branded-content deliverables with clear labeling.

These lanes must never be blended in one pitch or one KPI.

## Partnership modules

### 0. Invited Speaker / Content Guest

Media, platforms and organizers may invite Joyce for:

- keynote or practical operator session;
- fireside or interview;
- executive roundtable;
- private workshop or founder clinic;
- podcast, video or recurring content series.

The first conversation focuses on audience, topic and format. The positioning is a professional speaker/content collaboration, not a request for the outlet to provide PR.

### 1. Executive Roundtable Partner

Media contribution:

- nominate one editor/host/moderator;
- help shape a question relevant to its audience;
- optionally invite a small number of qualified readers/guests;
- optionally distribute a clearly labeled recap or recording.

GGC contribution:

- curated senior room;
- operator keynote and evidence memo;
- roundtable design and participant management;
- post-event anonymized insight summary.

### 2. 2027 Global Game Operator Outlook

Possible format:

- 12–20 page executive report;
- six charts or decision tables;
- ten falsifiable predictions with confidence/evidence state;
- 30-minute briefing or podcast;
- city salon launch.

Media role options:

- editorial preview under embargo;
- independent reporting or interview at the outlet’s discretion;
- commercial distribution partner under a separate agreement;
- co-host for a briefing or webinar;
- audience survey partner.

### 3. Operator Interview / Podcast Series

Possible episodes:

- AI Is Rebuilding the Game Business;
- How Global Games Actually Win;
- What Should We Build Next?;
- Soft Launch as a Decision System;
- 3 Seconds / 30 Minutes / Day 7;
- D2C and the New Game Commerce Stack.

The outlet’s existing format and audience take precedence. Do not send a generic interview request without confirming format fit.

### 4. City Intelligence Brief

After each salon, GGC can provide:

- top recurring founder questions;
- anonymized operating bottlenecks;
- category and market signals;
- decisions teams are postponing;
- partner/resource gaps;
- a five-action executive checklist.

This can become an independent story lead, a commissioned memo or a clearly labeled partner asset depending on the agreed lane.

### 5. Studio / Founder Clinic

- applications reviewed against a transparent rubric;
- 3–5 selected studios receive a short product/market diagnosis;
- media may observe or cover only with participant consent;
- confidential product details remain off-record unless explicitly cleared.

### 6. Live / Video / Newsletter Distribution

Options:

- livestream or recorded fireside;
- newsletter excerpt;
- short clips and quote cards;
- bilingual summary;
- event calendar placement;
- post-event AMA.

Every deliverable requires an owner, date, channel, label and usage-right agreement.

## Commercial deliverables menu

| Deliverable | Owner | Evidence/rights needed | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Co-hosted roundtable | GGC + media | participant consent, venue/recording terms | senior attendance, qualified follow-ups |
| Branded research | named partners | data license, methodology, review process | downloads, qualified readers, briefing requests |
| Branded newsletter | media | clear commercial label | delivered, clicks, qualified actions |
| Video/fireside | GGC + media | recording, editing, clip and archive rights | completion, shares, executive conversations |
| Audience survey | media + data partner | consent, question ownership, sample definition | usable sample, reportable findings |
| Founder clinic | GGC | application and confidentiality terms | qualified applicants, agreed next actions |

## Guardrails

- no guaranteed earned-media coverage;
- no unlabelled paid content;
- no sale of raw attendee/contact lists;
- no use of outlet or partner logos before written approval;
- no public client/project details without permission;
- no claim that a submission, meeting or commercial conversation equals endorsement;
- editorial rejection closes that format; commercial outreach cannot be used to bypass it;
- every publication, clip, photo and quote needs explicit rights.
