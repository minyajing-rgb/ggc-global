# GGC Sponsor Partnership Framework

Version: 2026-10-07 v1.0  
Status: public-safe working framework; commercial terms subject to written SOW.

## 1. Product definition

The GGC Global Game & AI Native Executive Salon is an invitation/application-based executive format designed to turn operator insight into trusted relationships, qualified business conversations, paid diagnostics, annual operating partnerships and reusable owner-controlled knowledge assets.

The product is deliberately small and decision-maker dense. It should not be sold as a mass conference, a generic founder meetup or a sponsored lecture tour.

## 2. Pilot structure

| Phase | City | Audience | Format | Go/no-go gate |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Online | 2 topic tests | 45–60 min briefing + CTA | Senior audience and qualified follow-up |
| 1 | Shenzhen | 30–60 | Keynote + partner module + roundtable + 1:1 | Senior density and committed partner support |
| 1 | Shanghai | 30–60 | Publishing / AI / adtech / D2C / global ops | Paid or material next-step signal |
| 1 | Beijing or Guangzhou | 30–60 | AI/platform/capital or mobile/studio focus | Chosen by strongest partner and audience pull |
| 2 | China expansion | 30–60/city | Repeatable local modules | Only after pilot economics pass |
| 3 | APAC | partner-hosted | Piggyback trusted organizer or anchor event | No self-produced APAC tour first |

## 3. Audience

Primary:

- game/App founders and CEOs;
- studio heads, GMs and publishing leaders;
- product, LiveOps, monetization, data and UA decision-makers;
- investors and platform/technology partners with operating relevance.

Not the primary audience:

- mass job-seeking traffic;
- generic AI curiosity without an operating mandate;
- vendors seeking attendee lists without substantive contribution;
- sponsors seeking guaranteed editorial coverage.

## 4. Sponsor stack and tailored value exchange

### A. Cloud / AI infrastructure

Targets: AWS for Games, Google Cloud for Games and credible AI infrastructure providers.

Partner problem:

- reach qualified studios before major infrastructure decisions;
- turn technical capability into concrete operator use cases;
- connect AI infrastructure with product, data, LiveOps and global deployment.

GGC offers:

- an operator-led AI Native Game Studio module;
- technical office hours or architecture clinic;
- qualified studio/project introductions with consent;
- anonymized operator questions and post-event insight memo;
- joint content showing where cloud/AI affects product and operating decisions.

GGC asks for:

- category-partner or founding-partner support;
- a senior game/AI expert, not only a sales presentation;
- credits, technical office hours, venue or cash support as appropriate;
- a written owner for post-event project qualification.

### B. Measurement / intelligence

Targets: AppsFlyer, Adjust and Sensor Tower.

Partner problem:

- help studios connect attribution, retention, monetization and market intelligence;
- create a credible point of view beyond product demos;
- reach senior teams before budget and market decisions are locked.

GGC offers:

- Soft Launch as a Decision System executive clinic;
- the 3 Seconds / 30 Minutes / Day 7 framework;
- a closed review of measurement blind spots;
- co-created benchmarks or a 2027 operator outlook;
- qualified demand and use-case feedback.

GGC asks for:

- measurement or intelligence category partnership;
- licensed charts/data where agreed, with attribution rules;
- an analyst/operator speaker and office hours;
- co-distribution or customer/community nominations.

Important separation: AppsFlyer/Adjust fit measurement and activation; Sensor Tower fits market intelligence and report collaboration. They should not receive the same email.

### C. Commerce / payments

Targets: Xsolla, Antom, WorldFirst and Stripe where fit.

Partner problem:

- connect global payment capability to the actual commercial design of games and Apps;
- educate teams on D2C, Web Shop, global acquiring, subscriptions, settlement, tax and compliance;
- source teams with a real international expansion need.

GGC offers:

- D2C and Global Monetization Lab;
- payment-readiness clinics using anonymized scenarios;
- founder/studio introductions with consent;
- content on payment conversion, market sequencing and operating ownership;
- optional referral or product-integration discussion after fit is confirmed.

GGC asks for:

- category or city partnership;
- a commercial/product expert and optional solutions clinic;
- partner support, customer cases and follow-through owner;
- cash, venue, data, credits or commercial referral terms as appropriate.

Fit differences:

- Xsolla: game-specific D2C, Web Shop, payments and partner network.
- Antom: global acquiring, digital entertainment and Asia/global payment scenarios.
- WorldFirst: business collections, cross-border settlement and partner/referral ecosystem; not positioned as a substitute for game checkout.
- Stripe: platform, subscription and software/payment infrastructure; best when tied to a real GGC or client product flow.

### D. Platforms / creative / distribution

Targets: Snap, TikTok, Meta and other credible platforms.

Partner problem:

- reach game/App decision-makers with more than a media-buying presentation;
- connect creative, discovery, acquisition, monetization and long-term player value;
- produce useful operator education and qualified product tests.

GGC offers:

- Creative to Cohort workshop;
- live creative diagnosis and experiment design;
- creator/community and performance-content discussions;
- post-event insight memo about studio needs and operating gaps;
- qualified test-project introductions with consent.

GGC asks for:

- platform/creative category partnership;
- local gaming/App business lead and solutions expert;
- workshop support, credits or test resources where appropriate;
- a named owner for qualified follow-ups.

### E. Local ecosystems

Targets: studios, founder communities, parks, associations and trusted organizers.

Partner problem:

- deliver a high-quality executive room, not a broad registration list;
- create locally relevant content and durable business follow-through;
- connect resident companies with global operating resources.

GGC offers:

- the content spine, operator keynote, selection rubric and follow-up system;
- partner-branded city module;
- anonymized problem map and post-event memo;
- optional founder/studio clinics.

GGC asks for:

- venue and local production support;
- qualified nominations and attendance confirmation;
- on-site relationship owner;
- local distribution and post-event follow-through.

## 5. Partnership tiers

### Founding Partner

- three-city pilot involvement;
- agenda input within GGC editorial/operating boundaries;
- agreed category exclusivity;
- senior role in one or more city modules;
- post-pilot review and first right to discuss the next phase.

### Category Partner

- one category problem across one or more cities;
- one substantive expert contribution;
- relevant co-branded assets and qualified follow-up;
- category-specific insight memo.

### City Host

- venue, production and local executive network;
- local module and community follow-through;
- city-level co-branding and post-event memo.

### Insight/Data Partner

- licensed data, analyst contribution and report collaboration;
- explicit source, use and attribution terms;
- no public claim beyond agreed data rights.

### Media Partner

- topic and audience collaboration;
- selected content capture/distribution where separately agreed;
- editorial independence preserved;
- sponsorship never guarantees editorial coverage.

### Community Partner

- qualified nominations;
- city/community distribution;
- follow-through with relevant founders and studios.

## 6. Rights and guardrails

- attendee data remains consent-based; no raw list resale;
- recording, photography, quote and logo rights require written agreement;
- category exclusivity must be explicit and scoped by city/term;
- data/benchmark use must include source, license and review terms;
- sponsorship and editorial coverage are separate lanes;
- all product claims and partner results require evidence;
- no client names, private code names or confidential delivery details enter public materials;
- no partner logo is used before written approval;
- no commercial price is implied by this framework.

## 7. Measurement

Minimum pilot dashboard:

- participant role and decision authority;
- source, city and partner attribution;
- qualified business problem;
- explicit next action and owner;
- opt-in contact status;
- partner-sourced and GGC-sourced conversations;
- direct cash and in-kind value;
- event-day contribution economics;
- content assets created and distribution results;
- 30/60-day business outcomes;
- partner repeat intent.

## 8. Standard next step

The first call is a 30-minute fit session:

1. partner priority for 2026–2027;
2. target audience and cities;
3. one business problem the partner can credibly own;
4. proposed expert/resource contribution;
5. desired business outcome;
6. measurement and follow-up owner;
7. whether to proceed to a written pilot SOW.
