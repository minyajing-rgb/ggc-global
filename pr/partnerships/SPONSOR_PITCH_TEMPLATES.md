## 9. Attachment rule

First touch:

- attach only the bilingual one-pager, or link to the executive profile;
- do not attach the full deck, rate card or private case material;
- do not attach the 28-page closed-door deck unless the recipient asks for content depth.

After human interest:

- one-pager;
- 8-page selected talk preview;
- short bio and 1–3 verified speaking links;
- proposed partner module;
- draft KPI/SOW only after fit is confirmed.

## 10. Sending discipline

- one company-specific reason;
- one collaboration ask;
- one next step;
- no guessed email address;
- no implied endorsement or already-agreed partnership;
- no bulk BCC;
- stop on rejection, opt-out or clear format mismatch;
- treat automated routing as routing, not interest.
