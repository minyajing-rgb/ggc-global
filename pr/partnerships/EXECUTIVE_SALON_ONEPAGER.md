## English

### What it is

GGC Global Game & AI Native Executive Salon is a curated, operator-led series for game and App founders, studio heads, publishing leaders and ecosystem partners navigating global markets.

The pilot begins with Shenzhen, Shanghai and Beijing or Guangzhou. Each room is intentionally limited to 30–60 participants, with at least 60% expected to be founders, CEOs, GMs, VPs or business owners.

This is not a generic conference tour. Each salon combines:

- one operator keynote with actionable decision frameworks;
- one partner module tied to a real business problem;
- a closed executive roundtable;
- curated 1:1 conversations;
- a post-event insight memo and reusable content assets.

### Core themes

1. **From China to Global** — market sequencing, product adaptation, publishing, monetization and operating ownership.
2. **AI Native Operator** — research, prototyping, validation, LiveOps and human-accountable decision systems.
3. **Capital, Commerce and Distribution** — cloud, measurement, payments, D2C, platforms, media and partner ecosystems.
4. **Local Market Module** — one city-specific topic shaped with the local partner.

Her career includes Microsoft, Tencent, Zynga and Gameloft, spanning product, analytics, LiveOps, monetization and global operations. ### What partners can do

- **Founding Partner** — shape the three-city pilot and the annual executive agenda.
- **Category Partner** — own one business problem: Cloud/AI, Measurement, Commerce, Platform/Creative, or Local Ecosystem.
- **City Host** — bring venue, local executive reach and a city-specific module.
- **Insight/Data Partner** — contribute licensed data, benchmarks, expert interpretation and a co-created report.
- **Media Partner** — help shape a reportable topic, reach the right audience and distribute selected public outputs, while preserving editorial independence.
- **Community Partner** — nominate qualified founders and studio leaders and support local follow-through.

### What partners receive

- direct access to a curated senior game/App audience;
- a substantive speaking or roundtable role, subject to topic fit;
- category exclusivity where agreed in writing;
- co-branded event and post-event assets;
- qualified opt-in introductions, never raw attendee-list resale;
- a partner-specific insight memo and agreed follow-up mechanism;
- optional product clinic, office hours, data workshop or customer case module;
- first right to discuss the next city after the pilot review.

### Pilot success measures

- at least 60% senior decision-makers;
- 15+ qualified business conversations per city;
- 5+ explicit follow-ups per city;
- 20+ opt-in direct relationships per city;
- 8–15 reusable content assets per keynote;
- at least one partner requesting the next city;
- direct costs covered by committed cash and in-kind value.

Commercial terms are scoped only after both sides agree on the pilot format.

## 中文

### 这是什么

GGC 全球游戏与 AI Native 高管闭门会，是面向游戏与 App 创始人、工作室负责人、发行负责人及生态伙伴的高密度实战系列。

首期计划从深圳、上海、北京或广州三城开始。每场严格控制在 30–60 人，预计至少 60% 为 Founder、CEO、GM、VP 或业务负责人。

它不是泛行业巡展，而是由真实操盘者主导的高质量决策场：

- 一场可落地的经营主题分享；
- 一个与真实业务问题直接相关的合作伙伴模块；
- 一场高管闭门圆桌；
- 精选的一对一交流；
- 一份会后洞察 Memo 与可复用内容资产。

### 固定主题

1. **From China to Global**：市场选择、产品适配、全球发行、商业化与经营责任。
2. **AI Native Operator**：研究、原型、验证、LiveOps 与保留人工责任的决策系统。
3. **Capital, Commerce and Distribution**：云、归因、支付、D2C、平台、媒体与生态合作。
4. **Local Market Module**：与每座城市的本地合作伙伴共同定义一个主题。

Joyce（闵雅婧）是 GGC 创始人，拥有 15+ 年游戏与产品经验，职业经历覆盖 Microsoft、腾讯、Zynga、Gameloft，长期连接产品、数据、LiveOps、商业化与全球运营。她曾在 Gameloft 与 10+ 工作室、500+ 跨职能协作网络共同推进业务，并著有《游戏运营与出海实战：策略、方法与技巧》（机械工业出版社，2024），拥有 2019–2026 年可公开核验的行业分享与专业输出记录。

### 合作方式

- **联合发起伙伴**：共同定义三城 Pilot 与年度高管议题。
- **品类伙伴**：围绕 Cloud/AI、归因与情报、支付与商业化、平台与创意、本地生态中的一个核心问题深度参与。
- **城市主办伙伴**：提供场地、本地高质量观众与城市主题模块。
- **数据/洞察伙伴**：提供合规可用的数据、基准和专家解读，共同形成行业报告。
- **战略媒体伙伴**：共同选择可报道议题、触达合适受众并分发公开成果，同时保持编辑独立。
- **社群伙伴**：推荐符合条件的创始人和业务负责人，并承接会后本地关系。

### 合作伙伴得到什么

- 与经过筛选的游戏/App高层直接交流；
- 与主题高度匹配的分享或圆桌席位；
- 经书面约定的品类排他；
- 联合品牌活动与会后内容资产；
- 基于明确同意的高质量引荐，不买卖原始参会名单；
- 一份合作伙伴专属洞察 Memo 与约定的会后动作；
- 可选产品门诊、Office Hours、数据工作坊或客户案例模块；
- Pilot复盘后下一城市的优先合作权。

### Pilot验收指标

- ≥60% 高层决策者；
- 每城 15+ 个合格业务交流；
- 每城 5+ 个明确后续动作；
- 每城 20+ 个用户主动同意的直接关系；
- 每场主题分享沉淀 8–15 个可复用内容资产；
- 至少一位合作伙伴愿意进入下一城；
- 现金与实物支持覆盖直接成本。
