## English executive profile
Joyce Mi is a global flagship game operator, founder of GGC Games, published author and enterprise operating system architect. Her career spans 15+ years in product and games, including Microsoft-related work, Tencent, Zynga and Gameloft, product leadership at Huogu and subsequent founder-led development and publishing ventures. At Zynga she served as Product Manager, leading and coordinating multiple producers and product managers across the Tencent edition of CityVille, the Chinese edition of FarmVille and global Bubble, Ruby and Slingo. At Gameloft she led Asian operations and the global operating work of Order & Chaos and Heroes of Order & Chaos, plus Minion Rush in China, coordinating 10+ studios and a 500+ cross-functional network, not direct reports.

Her work connects category judgment, product and revenue models, launch readiness, LiveOps, commercialization, international publishing and organizational execution. Joyce OS and GGC Principles codify these into transferable operating frameworks, not standards-body certification.
