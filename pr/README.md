## Public / private boundary

This repository is public.

Allowed here:
- sanitized biography and career claims already approved for public use;
- public-source speaking / publication / recognition records;
- approved headshots, book artwork and GGC-owned visual assets;
- talk abstracts, media topics, speaker proposals and public status tracking;
- public-facing outreach templates with no private recipient data.

Do **not** store here:
- journalist / producer private emails or phone numbers;
- private negotiation terms, travel reimbursement discussions or unpublished organizer correspondence;
- client names, confidential KPIs, contracts or private commercial terms;
- passport, home address, family, visa or identity-document data.

Private identity / administration records remain in the private `joyce-personal-records` repository. Private PR relationship data should stay in an authorized private business workspace rather than this public repository.

## Claim rule

A draft, submission or invitation is not an acceptance. A public biography claim must trace to the approved public master or the public newsroom/evidence ledger. First-party career records and third-party public evidence must remain distinguishable.

Do not solve evidence risk by flattening Joyce into a generic consultant or functional task list. Preserve the multi-category operator / operating-system-architect positioning, then choose the strongest wording supported for the specific public context under the positioning skill's evidence ladder.
