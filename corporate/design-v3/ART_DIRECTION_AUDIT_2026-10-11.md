## Selected creative direction
**GGC Editorial Aurora V3 = Operator Editorial (65%) + Biz Lucid Systems (25%) + SAGA/Universe human spark (10%).** Not a mathematically fixed rule; allocation describes relative prominence.

1. **Headline typography:** Modern bold grotesk sans for large Chinese/English text (Inter Display / PingFang SC / Noto Sans CJK or licensed equivalent). No conventional serif for body/H1/H2. Serif may appear only in a decorative Joyce signature/individual personal editorial treatment. Avoid Times-like serif for general B2B.
2. **Palette:** ink `#0D0C17`, warm white `#FFFEFA`, violet `#7438F4`, magenta `#FC1D91`, electric blue `#188CFF`, cyan `#28CDE6`, champagne gold `#CFA85E`. Accent pink/blue aurora and gold orbit details; don't paint every component with competing gradients.
3. **Hero:** 2/5–3/5 dominant oversized copy + authentic Joyce photo/art approved for external public display and lightweight orbit/halo, two audience-specific CTAs. Not a stack of UI mockup boxes or generic SaaS spheres.
4. **B2B proof:** Bright background, actual track record, product/system visualization with scoped claims. Don't borrow consumer AI/cosmic spiritual worlds as consultancy casework.
5. **Four pillars:** Minimal typographic architecture modules, readable two-line names, one job-to-be-done, real link. A clean system, not round-corner confetti.
6. **Product worlds:** Distinct real visual identity for Social Casino/Sims/Arcade, Bunny/consumer AI, Intelligence/AI Copilot, MEMO/lifestyle and knowledge platforms; each has a truthful release status and a source.
7. **Signature system:** Sparse fuchsia underlines, hand annotation as microaccent, golden orbital paths in Vision/Founder only, substantial negative space.
8. **Responsive:** 390px giant header legibility, vertical editorial rhythm, strong CTA, no overflowing hero or foreign-sized desktop portrait.
9. **Content rules:** Games, operators, products and verified outcomes first. Don't turn GGC into generic `AI for everyone`. Avoid unverified revenue, company counts, deal claims and false proof.
10. **Accessibility and QA:** Proper HTML text, not rasterized full-page banner; contrast and font-size tests, reduced motion, broken links, focus states and cross-page bilingual parity.
