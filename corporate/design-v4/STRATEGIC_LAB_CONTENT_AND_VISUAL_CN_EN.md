## 2. What clients need to decide / 六个核心问题

| Decision | Chinese buyer question | English buyer question | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foresight | 哪个赛道正在发生结构性变化？ | Where is meaningful change happening? | Opportunity thesis, contrary evidence, observation window and triggers |
| Strategic fit | 值得做，但是否适合我们做？ | Is it attractive — and right for us? | Capability/market fit, cost of entry, alternatives and stop conditions |
| Diagnosis | 表面问题后面，真正的瓶颈是什么？ | What is the real constraint? | Symptom-to-cause hypothesis map, evidence gaps and verification plan |
| Product & business | 产品循环、付费逻辑和经营模型成立吗？ | Does the product and business model hold? | Player value, retention/economy/cohort review, sensitivities |
| Capability & resources | 缺什么，自建、合作还是暂缓？ | What is missing: build, partner or defer? | Capability gap map, validated resource options, cost/time trade-offs |
| Execution | 下一步先做什么，谁负责，何时复盘？ | What happens next, who owns it, and how is it reviewed? | Sequenced actions, decision owner, experiment and review gates |

Never imply certain prediction or predetermined success. Specific forecasts require timestamps, assumptions, scenarios, confidence and invalidation conditions.

## 3. Insight is not just one person's opinion / 判断来自哪里

The latest user explains that GGC's research is informed partly by dialogue with people working inside leading companies and their views about product/category directions. Preserve this differentiated source of insight without implying formal corporate endorsement, guaranteed confidential access or distribution of private roadmaps.

Public wording:
“持续研究全球产品、商业模型与市场变化，并与头部企业的一线经营者和决策者交流。将行业认知与实际动作交叉验证，再结合贵司的产品、团队、预算与时间窗口形成判断。”
“Continuous research into global products, economics and market change, informed by dialogue with operators and decision-makers at leading companies. We test these perspectives against observable activity and your product, team, capital and timing.”

Four source layers:
1. Public/authorized market and product evidence.
2. Permissioned practitioner dialogue and experience.
3. Project-level operating knowledge and benchmarks, within disclosure rights.
4. Client-specific data, capabilities, budget, organization and constraints.

The differentiator is the synthesis and applicability, NOT the sale of leaked information. A speaker's opinion is not their employer's official strategy. No private conversations, identities, unreleased plans, NDA material or original private business records go onto the public website without explicit authorization.

### Illustrative scenario only — not a client result
Question: 自研团队应不应该立即搭建海外自发行？ / Should this studio build international self-publishing now?
Known scenario inputs: has an early playable; no reliable retention/economy cohorts yet; no accountable publishing owner. These are fictional illustrative premises.
Hypothesis to verify: first-session value/retention and operating capacity may be limiting factors, rather than simply lack of ad spend.
Recommendation under those premises: validate the product loop and economics; assign a publishing owner; run a narrow market test; defer full-scale acquisition.
Change condition: reconsider scaling when product, cohort economics, operating capacity and financial exposure meet agreed gates.
Resource option: compare a narrow specialist partner with building an entire publishing department; savings are not promised or pre-quantified.
Label throughout: 说明性场景 · 非真实客户结论 / Illustrative scenario · not a client case.

Interaction: select “新赛道 / 经营瓶颈 / 自研自发” and show a different illustrative memo. This is a portfolio of sample reasoning, not a live automated diagnosis. No private user data is submitted or analyzed.

## 9. Visual system / 工作名：Precision Foresight

Different from SAGA, Lingxi and previous Aurora proposals.

- Palette: cool white #F7F9FC, graphite #101620, cobalt #2457F5; optional restrained coral #FF5B55 for a constraint or action. No full rainbow gradients, pastel nebulae or golden mystic rings.
- Typography: modern sans-serif, strong but not cartoonishly inflated; no serif H1/H2, faux handwritten decoration or giant celebrity-style name.
- Layout: generous space, precise editorial grid, thin rules, well-proportioned research/decision surfaces. Rounded corners small and consistent rather than pill-shaped cards everywhere.
- Hero: approximately 55% strategic copy + 45% compact diagnostic memo; the memo is legible and includes a scenario label. GGC logo/name dominates identity; no human portrait.
- Graphic grammar: source markers, hypothesis labels, comparison matrices, capability gaps, decisions and review gates. Every visual element explains something.
- Motion proposal: selecting a decision question highlights the relevant evidence/constraint/action trace. No random particles or decorative fake live data.
- Futurism: conveyed by information precision, adaptive interaction and strong editorial confidence, not sci-fi scenery.
- Desktop/English and desktop/Chinese should be independently readable, not every line crammed in both languages at once. Mobile uses the same semantic sequence and equivalent translation.

## 11. Design acceptance / 验收标准

A first-time visitor should understand within one viewport: this is GGC, a company helping leaders make strategic decisions using research, operating experience and diagnosis; the next action is a discussion/sample, not a consumer AI chat.

Accept only when:
- Both languages say the same thing naturally.
- GGC/company capability is visually primary; founder identity is proof, not the main composition.
- Hero visualization communicates a real diagnostic method; all scenario premises are labeled illustrative.
- Research and product roles are distinguishable; no fake metrics, logos, testimonials, predictive guarantees or scientific performance claims.
- Cases tie decisions to role and scope; proprietary practitioner dialogue remains protected.
- Product directory still exists, but consumer art does not dominate the enterprise story.
- Diagnostic/contact action never falsely claims automated analysis, email delivery or a committed inquiry.
