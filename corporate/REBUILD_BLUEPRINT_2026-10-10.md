## Canonical positioning

**GGC — Global Game Copilot**  
Chinese: 打造好产品。做成全球生意。  
English: Build great products. Make them global.  
Vision: Capital · Flow · Prosperity  
Brand promise: Good Games. A Brighter World.

GGC connects global gaming and AI-native product strategy, operating systems, talent, capital and distribution. Four mutually reinforcing businesses: 1) Principal-led services and operating partnerships, 2) AI Copilot software and intelligence, 3) co-development, joint greenlight/publishing and strategic capital, 4) owned products and direct-to-consumer IP. Global partner network is the cross-cutting ecosystem, **not** a fifth unrelated consultancy pillar.

## Why the rebuild

- Root domain currently reads as one person's AI-assisted game consulting site. It includes outdated capability totals, unsupported performance promises and off-message language.
- `biz.ggcgames.com` is a much richer, public-scope Joyce business site: 16 services and 14 capability domains, but the landing page still places Joyce before the enterprise architecture.
- `opsgo.ggcgames.com` is a separate content/education/insight surface and has public numerical claims requiring verification.
- This corporate prototype makes the enterprise, operating model, products, company development, partnership and updates visible above the founder proof.
- "10+ studios / 500+ cross-functional collaborators" is **not** the same as ten owned companies or 500 direct reports. Project tiles are NOT a legal subsidiary register.

## Recommended live architecture

- `ggcgames.com/` — corporate hub: four businesses / vision / news / partners / portfolio.
- `ggcgames.com/services/` — acquisition front for engagements; detailed rate card can remain on `biz.ggcgames.com/joyce/`.
- `ggcgames.com/copilot/` — product directory with clear product maturity labels and links to independently hosted tools; `biz.ggcgames.com/sme/` remains immediate existing source.
- `ggcgames.com/co-build/` — founder/studio/publisher/investor co-build routes.
- `ggcgames.com/portfolio/` — game & AI project directory, owned vs. partner vs. research clearly marked.
- `ggcgames.com/about/` — origin, past/present/future, governance, team and Joyce / book.
- `ggcgames.com/insights/` and `news/` — aggregated editorial and verified PR; link to OpsGo as source.
- `ggcgames.com/partners/` and `contact/` — purpose-based structured intake.
- `ggcgames.com/subscribe/` — confirmed opt-in newsletter.
- `biz.ggcgames.com/joyce/` — dedicated executive profile/Why Joyce, not canonical corporate homepage.
- `opsgo.ggcgames.com/` — research, education, knowledge and newsletter editorial source.
- Product apps on existing domains remain independently deployed. D2C product storefront and payment should stay separated from B2B lead intake.
