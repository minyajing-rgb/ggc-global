## Operating rules

- Deadline-driven opportunities are executed in order of expiry and strategic fit.
- A portal form, payment step, consent screen or draft is not a submission; a submission requires a receipt, confirmation page or organizer email.
- A submitted application is not an acceptance, finalist selection, publication or award.
- No automatic bulk outreach or automatic follow-up is active for this PR pipeline.
- Media selection requires a specific route, format and dated fit evidence. A format rejection closes the pitch and suppresses materially similar outlet-format proposals across contacts; an automated routing response is not a positive editorial reply. Apply [Media Route Feedback](pr/authority-engine/MEDIA_ROUTE_FEEDBACK.md) and the [Outreach Playbook](pr/authority-engine/OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md).
- Keep independent editorial separate from paid PR, branded content, sponsorship and BD / marketing partnerships. No paid PR purchase is authorized by a media-graph update.
- Public-facing claims must distinguish first-party operating records from independently verified third-party evidence.
- Private phone numbers, financial models, internal client records and raw source archives remain outside this public tracker.
- Profile country/region uses the applicable Hong Kong option where relevant. Nationality is handled as a separate field and is never inferred.
