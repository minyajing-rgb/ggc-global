# 星空连线 · Star Connect — Public Playtest

**[▶ Play free in your browser](https://star-connect-ggc-preview.vercel.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=starconnect_launch&utm_content=ggc_repo)**

A light, mobile-first constellation puzzle from **GGC Games**. No sign-up, no download, no in-app purchase. **120 campaign levels**, plus daily and endless challenges.

## What changes from level 1 to level 5?

| Level | Distinct rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Touch numbered stars in order |
| 2 | Collect a star crystal while tracing the constellation |
| 3 | Find a key before crossing a locked gate; moving star |
| 4 | Complete two colored routes while avoiding a black hole |
| 5 | Chase rotating stars and collect two crystals within the timer |

More levels introduce these mechanics in new layouts, with progression stored locally on the device.

## Help us improve it

Try to finish the first five levels, then share feedback on: (1) which screen confused you, (2) whether touch targets were large enough, and (3) which mechanic was most fun. **The public browser test is live.** WeChat/Douyin mini-game release, platform ad inventory and official review remain separate and are **not yet live**.
